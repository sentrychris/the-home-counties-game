A US Army carbine. Before the strikes, NATO moved men and kit through Britain by the convoy load, and when the convoys stopped, a good deal of it never left the depots. Crates of M4s went missing along with everything else.

They're rarer on Duncombe Street than the AK, and so is their ammunition: 5.56 turns up in bandoleers and the odd box, never by the tin. About one scav in eight carries one.

It's lighter than an AK, kinder on the shoulder and quicker to the eye, but it likes being kept clean. Carl gets a few in, cleaned and test-fired, and he'll buy back any black-tipped rounds you find.
