Nobody is sure where all the AKs on Duncombe Street came from. The army's version involves a container at the Bletchley rail yard that was waiting to be shipped elsewhere; when the trains stopped, it stayed where it was. Locals tell a different story, about rifles kept under floorboards and in lofts by people who had been expecting trouble for years.

Either way, by the second winter most scav groups between the railway and Oliver Road had a few, and 7.62 ammunition was never hard to find.

The rifle is heavy and kicks hard, but it keeps working after being left out in a wet garden, and that's what people here value most.
