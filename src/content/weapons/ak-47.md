Russian-pattern, with the serial number ground off. Before the strikes, Russian money found its way to criminal gangs and dissident groups all over Britain, and rifles like this one came with it. Not everyone who took them believed in anything. Most just wanted the money.

When the government left Bletchley, the guns stayed. Most have changed hands several times since: sold, swapped, or taken off the dead. Every scav crew between the railway and Oliver Road has a few, and 7.62 ammunition is never hard to find.

The rifle is heavy and kicks hard, but it keeps working after being left out in a wet garden, and that's what people here value most. Carl, who used to be a Royal Marine, sells them test-fired, along with the parts to build them up.
