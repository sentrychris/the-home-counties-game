You have **440 HP**, shown in the bottom-left corner of the HUD under a figure of your body.

## Where you've been hit

When you take damage, the figure shows **which part of you** was hit: head, chest, arms or legs. Head and chest hits are the most dangerous, which is what helmets and armour are for.

> If the figure starts lighting up, stop looting, get into cover and heal.

## Healing

Press <kbd>H</kbd> to heal with the medical items you're carrying. Healing takes time, so do it somewhere out of sight, such as behind a wall, inside a house or behind a shed, rather than out in the car park.

Medical items on Duncombe Street:

- **Bandages.** Small, light and cheap. Worth carrying a couple every raid.
- **Painkillers.** Take the edge off pain so you can keep moving.
- **First aid kits.** Heal more, but they're bigger, heavier and worth more.

Keep meds in your pockets or rig, where they're quick to reach, rather than in your backpack.

## Staying alive

- **Don't heal in the open.** A scav that has just shot you knows where you are.
- **Break line of sight first.** Get round a corner, then heal.
- **Watch your stamina.** If you're out of breath, you can't sprint away.
- **Wear a helmet**, even a basic one.
