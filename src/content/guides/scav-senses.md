Scavs notice you by **what they hear** and **what they see**. Once they've noticed you, they don't forget straight away.

## Sound

Everything you do makes some noise. Roughly, loudest first:

1. **Gunfire.** Every scav nearby hears it, and can tell which direction it came from.
2. **Sprinting.** Loud footsteps.
3. **Running.** Normal movement. Fine when nobody's close.
4. **Walking** (<kbd>Caps Lock</kbd>). Much quieter. Use it when scavs may be nearby.
5. **Crouch-walking** (<kbd>C</kbd>). The quietest, and the slowest.

Some ground is noisier than others. **The railway is noisy**, and sound carries a long way across the open ground by the tracks.

> From the field copy: watch the railway, you can hear movement for miles.

## Sight

Scavs see you when you're in their line of sight and close enough to make out. The more of you that's showing, and the more you're moving, the sooner they notice.

- **Open ground is risky.** The car park has no cover, and the field copy marks a lookout in the middle of it.
- **Fences, hedges and walls block line of sight.** The back gardens are slower to move through, but you can't be spotted from the road.
- **Long grass and bushes help**, but they don't hide you completely. A scav looking straight at you will see you.

## Cover and leaning

**Cover** is anything that stops bullets: brick walls, cars, the corners of houses. **Concealment** only hides you: hedges, grass, a wooden fence. It's worth knowing which one you're behind.

**Lean** with <kbd>Q</kbd> and <kbd>E</kbd> to look round corners. Leaning shows a scav your head and shoulder; stepping out shows all of you.

## What scavs do

- **They investigate.** If they hear something, they come and look. A shot from the gardens draws them towards the gardens.
- **They take cover.** Shoot at a scav and it gets behind something before shooting back.
- **They flank.** When there's more than one, expect one to keep you pinned while another moves round the side.
- **They search.** If they lose sight of you, they search where they last saw or heard you.

The easiest fights are the ones where scavs never know you're there. If a fight does start, moving after every few shots makes you harder to pin down.
