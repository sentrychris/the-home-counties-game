Press <kbd>Tab</kbd> to open your gear. Everything you wear goes into a slot, and each slot holds one item.

## The slots

| Slot | What goes in it | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Helmet | Head protection | K6-3 helmet, MICH helmet |
| Armour | Body armour | Plate carrier |
| Rig | Chest rig for magazines and meds | Chest rig |
| Backpack | Where most of your loot goes | Small assault pack, large rucksack |
| Primary | Your main rifle | AK-47 |
| Secondary | A second rifle | AK-47 |
| Holster | Sidearm | Browning Hi-Power |
| Knife | Melee | Ka-Bar knife |
| Pockets (4) | Small items | Ammo, bandages, painkillers |

Swap between your two rifles with <kbd>1</kbd> and <kbd>2</kbd>.

## Inventory space

Inside a backpack or rig, items take up space on a grid. Small items like bandages and painkillers take one square; an AK-47 takes five by two. A bigger backpack holds more, but weighs more when empty.

The [gear and items database](../../gear/) lists sizes, weights and values.

## Weight

Your carry weight is shown in the bottom-left corner of the HUD. Armour, a full backpack and two rifles add up quickly. The more you carry, the slower you move and the faster your stamina drains.

> A car battery is worth £150 but weighs 12 kg. Lighter valuables, like a gold watch or an old phone, are usually a better use of the space.

## What to take

- **Early on:** one rifle, ammo in your pockets, a bandage and a small backpack. Cheap to lose.
- **Once you have more kit:** add a helmet and a rig. Armour is the heaviest thing you'll wear, and the most likely to keep you alive.
- **Every raid:** something to heal with, and room to bring loot back.
