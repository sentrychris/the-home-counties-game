Your gear is set up in the hideout's **Storage** tab, and in a raid <kbd>Tab</kbd> opens it. Everything you wear goes into a slot, and each slot holds one item.

## The slots

| Slot | What goes in it | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Helmet | Head protection | K6-3 helmet, MICH helmet |
| Armour | Body armour | Plate carrier |
| Rig | Chest rig for magazines, grenades and meds | Chest rig |
| Backpack | Where most of your loot goes | Small assault pack, large rucksack |
| Primary | Your main rifle | AK-47 |
| Secondary | A second rifle | AK-47 |
| Holster | Sidearm | Browning Hi-Power |
| Knife | Melee | Ka-Bar knife |
| Pockets (4) | One-square items | Ammo, bandages, painkillers, grenades |

Swap between your two rifles with <kbd>1</kbd> and <kbd>2</kbd>.

## Inventory space

Inside a backpack or rig, items take up space on a grid. Small items like bandages and painkillers take one square, a magazine takes two (one wide, two tall, so it won't go in a pocket), and an AK-47 takes five by two. The chest rig holds 4x2, the small assault pack 4x4 and the large rucksack 5x6. A bigger backpack holds more, but weighs more when empty.

## Moving things about

- **Drag** an item to move it. Drop it on a slot or square that's already taken and the two **swap**.
- **Right-click** for the item's menu, which opens by the mouse: use, open, read, load, unload, drop and so on.
- **Double-click** a rig or backpack, or right-click it and pick **Open**, to open it in its own window. Bags go inside bags: a rig in a small pack in a rucksack, each one openable, and a full rig keeps what's in it.
- <kbd>X</kbd> drops whatever's under the mouse in a raid.
- In the hideout, **AUTO-ORGANISE** packs the stash tight in a sensible order. Otherwise it keeps things where you put them.

## Magazines and ammo

The AK takes 30-round magazines, and each magazine holds one kind of round. To load one, drag a pile of rounds onto it, or right-click it and pick **Load with...**. To fit one, drag it onto the rifle or right-click the rifle and pick **Load magazine...**. In a raid, <kbd>R</kbd> swaps in your fullest magazine of the same ammo and <kbd>T</kbd> one with the next kind. Keep spare magazines in your rig: from a backpack it takes longer.

The [gear and items database](../../gear/) lists sizes, weights and values.

## Weight

Your carry weight is shown at the top of your gear, and a weight icon appears beside the body figure on the HUD when you're overloaded. Armour, a full backpack and two rifles add up quickly. The more you carry, the slower you move and the faster your stamina drains.

> A car battery is worth £150 but weighs 12 kg. Lighter valuables, like a gold watch or an old phone, are usually a better use of the space.

## What to take

- **Early on:** one rifle with a couple of loaded magazines, a bandage and a small backpack. Cheap to lose. **Pack essentials** on the Storage tab fills your rig and pockets for you.
- **Once you have more kit:** add a helmet and a rig. Armour is the heaviest thing you'll wear, and the most likely to keep you alive.
- **Every raid:** something to heal with, and room to bring loot back.
- **When you can afford it:** a grenade or two in the rig. A smoke covers a dash across the road; a frag clears a room. See [Grenades](../grenades/).
