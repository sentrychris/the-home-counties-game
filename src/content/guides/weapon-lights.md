A weapon light lights up dark rooms, stairwells and the inside of the old Sainsbury's, and it lets every scav in front of you know exactly where you are. Press <kbd>T</kbd> to turn it on or off.

## Fitting one

A light needs a rail, and neither gun has one as it comes. Do it all at the [gunsmith](../gunsmith/):

- **AK-47.** Swap the wooden lower handguard for the **AK railed lower handguard** (£380, 0.26 kg, ergonomics -2). It opens a **Left rail** and an **Under rail**, and each takes a light, so you can run two.
- **Browning Hi-Power.** Fit the **Hi-Power rail adapter** (£120, 0.05 kg, ergonomics -1), which clamps round the front of the trigger guard, then a compact light under the barrel.

Pick the light on the bench to see it on the gun, and anything you haven't got is bought from Carl when you assemble. Take the railed handguard off and any light on it comes off too.

## When to use it

- **Indoors.** The houses have no working lights, and the back rooms and upstairs are gloomy even by day. The old Sainsbury's is darker still.
- **At night.** On a night raid the houses and the store are pitch black inside. Night raids are being tested in the developer menu for now.
- **Not when you're hiding.** Scavs spot you up to two and a half times as fast when they're in your beam, and in the dark they see you from as far off as they would by day. Turn it off before you look round a corner, and on when you're ready to go in.

In clear air the beam itself is invisible: scavs see the light, not a cone. In mist and smoke there's a faint cone that shows where you're pointing.

In a squad, everyone sees your light, and pointing it straight at a squadmate dazzles them.

## Where to get them

**Carl** sells railed handguards, Hi-Power rail adapters and compact lights from the start, though not every restock. Scout lights turn up once he knows you, and always once he trusts you. Now and then a scav has a railed handguard on its AK.
