These are the default keys in the current prototype. You can change any of them in the game under **Settings > Controls**: click a key, then press the new one. Each control can have two keys, and if you pick one that's already in use, the two controls swap.

## Keys worth knowing

- <kbd>Caps Lock</kbd> **walk.** Slower than running, and much quieter. Useful when scavs are nearby.
- <kbd>Q</kbd> and <kbd>E</kbd> **lean.** Look round a corner or a fence post without stepping out, so less of you is exposed.
- <kbd>R</kbd> **reload.** Swaps in your fullest magazine of the same ammo, from your rig and pockets first. A magazine from your backpack takes longer.
- <kbd>Y</kbd> **next ammo.** Swaps in a magazine loaded with the next kind of ammo you're carrying.
- <kbd>T</kbd> **weapon light.** Turns the light on your rifle or pistol on and off. See [Weapon lights](../weapon-lights/).
- <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd> **check ammo.** The HUD doesn't show your ammo all the time. This shows the rounds in the magazine and what you carry of that type for a few seconds, then fades.
- <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>I</kbd> **inspect.** Lifts your rifle and turns it to show both sides.
- <kbd>3</kbd> **pistol.** Draws the Hi-Power from your holster. One shot each time you pull the trigger.
- <kbd>4</kbd> **knife.** Left mouse slashes, right mouse stabs: slower, but it hits much harder.
- <kbd>G</kbd> **grenade.** Brings out a grenade from your rig or pockets. See the [Grenades](../grenades/) guide.
- <kbd>1</kbd> and <kbd>2</kbd> **swap rifles.** You can carry two: one in the primary slot and one in the secondary.
- <kbd>C</kbd> **crouch.** Press once to crouch and again to stand.
- <kbd>Space</kbd> **jump or vault.** Most garden fences and low walls can be vaulted, which is often quicker than going round.
- <kbd>F</kbd> **search, open and read.** Tap F to read a paper where it lies; hold it to take the paper home for the board in your hideout.
- <kbd>X</kbd> **drop.** In the gear screen, drops whatever's under the mouse.
- <kbd>F12</kbd> **hide the HUD.** Clears everything off the screen for a clean view. Press it again to bring it all back.

## Photo mode

Playing on your own, the Esc menu has a **Photo mode** button. Hold the right mouse button to look round, move the camera with your movement keys, <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>E</kbd> to go up and <kbd>C</kbd> or <kbd>Q</kbd> to go down. <kbd>P</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd> takes the photo, <kbd>H</kbd> or <kbd>F12</kbd> hides the panel, <kbd>R</kbd> resets the camera and <kbd>Esc</kbd> goes back.

## Crosshair

There's no crosshair by default: you aim with your sights. Turn it on under **Settings > Game**.
