The game starts in your hideout. Everything between raids happens here, through the tabs along the top bar. Your cash, and how many raids you've done, got out of and been killed in, are in the top right.

## Raid

Pick a map (Duncombe Street for now, or the Practice yard to try things out), check **Your kit** and what it's worth, and press **Start raid**. You're asked to confirm, then the raid loads behind a loading screen with tips. In a squad, only the host can start, once everyone's back. Each raid gets its own weather and time of day.

## Storage

Your gear on the left and your stash on the right. Drag things between them, right-click for an item's menu, double-click a bag to open it, and use **Pack essentials** to fill your rig and pockets with loaded magazines and meds, or **Empty pockets and bags** to put it all back. **AUTO-ORGANISE** tidies the stash. More in [Loadout and gear](../loadout-and-gear/).

## Traders

Four traders to buy from, sell to and do jobs for: Chalky, Carl, Crapper and Sofie. Each has their own stock, prices, notes and standing. See [Traders](../../traders/).

## Gunsmith

Your rifle on a bench, to fit a sight, mount, muzzle, stock and magazine and see how it changes the rifle's handling. See [the Gunsmith guide](../gunsmith/).

## Papers

A cork board for the papers you've brought home from raids: leaflets, logs, letters, notes and a newspaper. Each one you take stays pinned up for good, and the board shows how many of them you've found. Click one to read it.

## Squad, Settings and quitting

The icons on the right of the top bar open the **Squad** box (see [Co-op squads](../co-op-squads/)), **Settings** and **Quit**. Settings has five pages:

- **Game:** mouse sensitivity, field of view and the list of keys.
- **Display:** window mode, resolution, FSR upscaling, VSync, a frame-rate limit and the Menu size.
- **Graphics:** the overall quality, how far grass is drawn, anti-aliasing, ambient occlusion, texture sharpness and sun shadows.
- **Picture:** Colours, from Natural to Gritty (darker and more muted, like Tarkov), and camera effects you can switch on and off: film grain, sharpening, vignette, colour fringing, sun glare and injury effects.
- **Sound:** master, effects, voices, background (wind, birds and the far-off war) and music volumes.

The Character, Map and Tasks tabs are greyed out for now. They're coming in later builds.
