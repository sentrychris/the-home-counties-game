There are three grenades on Duncombe Street, all US Army issue from the NATO stores that came through Britain.

| Grenade | Fuse | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **M67 frag** | 4.2 s | A blast within 4 m and 900 fragments. Each body part it can see is struck by its share: a few at 2 m, one in three at 10 m, rarely at 20 m. Walls, cars and corners save you, and helmets and plates stop most of it. Stuns within 12 m |
| **M84 flashbang** | 1.6 s | Whites you out for up to five seconds if you're close and looking at it, a third of that looking away, with ringing ears for up to eight. It won't kill anyone |
| **M18 smoke (grey)** | 1.4 s | Pours grey smoke for 55 seconds and hangs about 30 more, drifting downwind. It grows into a wall about 9 m across that scavs can't see through |

## Carrying them

Grenades go in your **rig or pockets**, one square each. The backpack is too slow to get at, as in Tarkov. Carl sells smoke from the start, and flashbangs and frags once he knows you. Scavs carry them too, so check their rigs.

## Throwing

1. Press <kbd>G</kbd> to take one out. The rifle goes down and the grenade comes up in your right hand. Press <kbd>G</kbd> again for the next kind you're carrying. The ammo box in the bottom right shows which one and how many you have.
2. **Hold the left mouse button.** Your left hand pulls the pin and your arm draws back, ready.
3. **Let go** to throw it overarm. It leaves your hand about 15 degrees above where you're looking.

Use the **right mouse button** instead to lob it underhand, for a short, soft throw round a corner or through a doorway. <kbd>1</kbd> or <kbd>2</kbd> puts it away with the pin still in.

**How far:** an M67 thrown looking level lands about 26 m away, and looking 20 degrees up about 37 m, its best. The lighter flashbang goes a little further, the heavier smoke a little shorter. Underhand, looking down a bit, is 6 or 7 metres. Your own speed carries into the throw.

**Where it ends up:** what it lands on matters. Concrete, wood and metal give a lively bounce and a roll of a few metres; cars the liveliest. Gravel, grass and hedges stop it within a metre or so. The flashbang and smoke cans roll round and stop sooner than the frag.

## Cooking a frag

With a frag's pin out and the button still held, press <kbd>R</kbd>. The spoon flies off with a ping and the fuse starts burning in your hand, so the grenade goes off sooner after it lands, or in the air over someone's head. There's no timer on the screen: count it yourself. Hold it too long and it goes off in your hand. A flashbang's fuse is too short to cook, and there's no point cooking a smoke.

If you die holding a grenade with the pin out, it drops at your feet.

## The scavs and their grenades

- They **run for cover** from a frag or flashbang they see land or hear clink nearby.
- Holding an angle, they now and then **lob one at where they last saw you**, from 7 to 28 metres.
- Falling back, they often **pop smoke** between you and them first.
- A flashbang blinds and dazes them as it does you, and makes them drop a grenade they were about to throw.

## Tips

- A smoke between you and a lookout covers a dash across the road. Remember it hides them from you too.
- A frag through a doorway or window clears a room; the blast blows windows in if it's close.
- In a squad everyone sees the same grenade, so say where yours is going.
