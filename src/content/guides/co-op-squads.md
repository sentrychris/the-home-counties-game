Up to four players can raid together. One of you hosts, and the host's game runs the raid for everyone. You join through **Steam**, so there's no port forwarding and nothing extra to install.

## Setting up a squad

1. **Open Steam** before you start the game. Everyone needs the same build of the game.
2. In the hideout, click the **Squad** icon in the top bar.
3. **Host a squad.** This makes a friends-only Steam lobby.
4. Your Steam friends who are in the game are listed in the Squad box. **Invite** them, or use **Invite through Steam**. They can also click **Join** next to your name in their own Squad box, accept the Steam invite, or use **Join Game** in their Steam friends list.

Your Steam name is your squad name unless you change it. A friend on a different build is turned away before they connect, and the Squad box shows which build you're on.

There's also **Same PC or network**, for joining by address on a home network.

## Going in

Everyone keeps their own hideout, stash, cash and traders. Each of you takes your own kit in and extracts into your own stash.

Only the host can start a raid, once everyone's back in the hideout. Then you all go in together, with the same map, start, weather and time of day, standing side by side.

## In the raid

- **Your squadmates** wear a near-black tracksuit (scavs wear white), in their own gear, with their name over their head within 60 metres. You hear their footsteps and shots.
- **Scavs** are run by the host's game for everyone, with two extra for each friend who came. Each one goes after whichever of you it can see, and turns on whoever shoots it.
- **Friendly fire is on.** Watch your angles.
- **Loot is shared.** Everyone sees the same crates, bodies and dropped items. One of you searches a thing at a time; anyone else who tries is told who's in it. What you take is gone for the others.
- **No pause.** Esc opens the menu, but the raid carries on round you.

## When it's over

Each of you extracts, dies or leaves on your own, and the others are told.

- **If you're killed**, your body stays where you fell with everything you had, and your squad can search it with <kbd>F</kbd> and carry your kit out for you. It's still lost to you.
- **Once you're out**, the raid summary offers **Watch squadmates**: a camera over a friend's shoulder. Click or Space for the next one, right-click for the one before, Esc to go back.
- **If the host leaves** while others are still in the raid, they're asked first. Then it's over for everyone: whoever's still in keeps what they have, counted as an extraction. The same happens if the host's game closes or the connection drops.
