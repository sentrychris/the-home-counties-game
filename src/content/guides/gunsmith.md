The **GUNSMITH** tab in the hideout is where you build your guns: the AK-47 and the Browning Hi-Power. You can also right-click a gun anywhere and pick **Modify at the gunsmith**.

## Using the bench

1. **Pick a gun** from **Your guns** on the left. It lists every rifle and pistol you have, wherever it is: in your hands, slung, holstered, in your bag or in the stash.
2. **Look it over.** Drag to turn it, right-drag to move it, scroll to zoom, and double-click to put it back.
3. **Pick a slot**, by its card or by clicking the part on the rifle. Its parts are listed on the left with what they do and where they'd come from.
4. **Try a part.** Point at one to see it on the rifle and how the stats change: green is better, red is worse. Click it to put it on the bench.
5. **Assemble.** Nothing changes until you do. Parts come from your stash first, then your bag, and anything you haven't got is bought from Carl, after a box asks you. Parts that come off go in your stash. **Reset** puts the bench back as it was.

## The slots

| Slot | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| Sight | None (iron sights), the open red dot, or the 4x prism scope. Either sight needs the mount, and picking one adds it |
| Mount | None, or the AK side rail mount |
| Muzzle | Bare, or the 7.62 compact suppressor |
| Handguard | The wooden lower handguard the AK comes with, or the railed one. There's always one fitted |
| Left rail, Under rail | A weapon light. They need the railed handguard |
| Stock | The wooden stock the AK comes with, or none |
| Magazine | Any magazine from your stash or bag, a new empty one from Carl, or none |

The Hi-Power has three slots: **Rail** (none, or the clamp-on rail adapter), **Light** (a compact light, once the rail is on) and **Magazine**.

## The stats

Bars out of 100, longer is better:

- **Ergonomics** (46 as it comes). How quickly you raise the sights and how steady you hold them. Each point under 46 makes aiming 1.5% slower and the sway 1.5% bigger, and each point over makes it quicker and steadier.
- **Vertical recoil control** (52) and **horizontal recoil control** (58). How hard it kicks up and to the side.
- **Accuracy** (40). How tightly the rounds group.

And the rest:

- **Muzzle velocity** (715 m/s), **effective range** (300 m; beyond it, hits lose some damage), **fire rate** (600 rounds a minute) and **weight**.

The Hi-Power as it comes: ergonomics 78, recoil control 64 / 62, accuracy 34, 350 m/s, an effective range of 50 m, and semi-automatic, one shot each time you pull the trigger.

The AK as it comes shoots exactly as it always has. The parts change it like this:

The prices are what the parts are worth; Carl adds his markup when he sells them. A bare AK, without its stock and lower handguard, is worth £1,010 and weighs 2.85 kg; as it comes, £1,200 and 3.5 kg. A rifle's worth and weight include its parts, and it sells with them.

## Looking through the sights

The 4x scope is **picture-in-picture**: aiming brings the eyepiece up to your eye and zooms your view only a little, while inside the eyepiece you see four times closer. Your view turns more slowly while you're looking through it. The red dot comes up a touch higher than the iron sights with the same slight zoom.

## Where to get parts

- **Carl** sells a stock and a mount from the start, and now and then a red dot or a suppressor. The scope and suppressor turn up by chance once he knows you, and always once he trusts you. Railed handguards, Hi-Power rail adapters and compact lights are on his list from the start, and scout lights once he knows you. He pays best for parts, too.
- **Lights** are explained in [Weapon lights](../weapon-lights/).
- **Crates** sometimes hold a part.
- **Scavs** sometimes carry a suppressor, a scope, a red dot, a bare mount or a railed handguard on their rifles, and now and then a rifle with no stock.
