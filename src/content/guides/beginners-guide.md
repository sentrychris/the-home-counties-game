The Home Counties is an extraction shooter. You go into a raid with some gear and try to come out with more. If you die, you lose what you took in.

## The raid loop

1. **Gear up.** Choose what to take: a weapon, armour if you have it, a backpack for loot, and some meds. Everything you take in is at risk.
2. **Deploy.** You start at one of two points on Water Eaton Road, at the south of the map: the west end by the railway bridge, or the east end by the roadworks. The game picks one at random.
3. **Loot.** Search crates, open houses and dead scavs with <kbd>F</kbd>. Your carry weight is shown in the bottom-left corner.
4. **Extract.** Reach one of the two exits before the raid timer runs out, and stay in the green zone for ten seconds.

A raid on Duncombe Street lasts **12 minutes**. The countdown is in the top-right corner of the screen.

## Getting out

Both exits are at the north end of the map, a long way from both starts.

- **Station Footpath**, north-west corner (grid B1). The lane up to Bletchley station.
- **Oliver Road**, north-east corner (grid I1). By the hoarding at the old Sainsbury's.

Each exit is an extraction zone with a **red smoke flare**. If you lose your bearings, look for the smoke above the rooftops. To extract, walk into the green zone and stay there for ten seconds.

> Work out where the nearest smoke is early in the raid, not when the timer is nearly out.

## What you lose when you die

If you're killed, or the timer reaches zero before you extract, **you lose everything you took into the raid and everything you picked up in it**: weapon, armour, backpack and loot.

The usual advice for extraction shooters applies: **only take in what you can afford to lose.**

## Your first few raids

- **Go in light.** A rifle, some ammo and a bandage. If the raid goes wrong, you haven't lost much.
- **Learn one exit.** Run to it and extract a few times, then try it from the other start.
- **Stay off the road where you can.** The gardens are slower, but you can't be seen from a hundred metres away.
- **Leave time to get out.** Head for an exit with a few minutes still on the clock.
- **Use the map.** The [tactical map](../../map/) shows every crate spot, scav spawn and open house.

## Reading the HUD

| Where | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Top right | Time left in the raid |
| Bottom left, figure | Where you've been hit |
| Bottom left, HP | Your health, out of 440 |
| Bottom left, weight | How much you're carrying, in kg |
| Bottom left, bar | Stamina. Sprinting drains it |
| Bottom right | Rounds in the magazine / rounds you're carrying, and the ammo type (FMJ) |
