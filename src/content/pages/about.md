## The game

The Home Counties is an extraction shooter, played alone or in a squad of up to four. You go into a raid with a loadout, search houses, crates and fallen scavs, fight armed scavs who use cover, flank and search for you, and need to reach an exit before the raid timer runs out. If you die or run out of time, you lose everything you were carrying. Between raids you're in your hideout: trading with four traders you never meet, building your rifle at the gunsmith, and pinning up the papers you've brought home.

## The story so far

Russia launched a full-scale invasion into Europe, and Britain became NATO's logistics hub. American and allied troops, vehicles, weapons and armour poured through its ports, airbases and depots.

Britain was already divided, worn down by COVID, heatwaves, cyberattacks and one political crisis after another. Russia saw a chance to cut the link between Europe and the US, and quietly funded criminal gangs and dissident groups, each with its own agenda.

The Home Counties, the ring of counties around London, were meant to be a protective barrier for the capital and a Civilian Protection and Continuity Zone for people displaced by the fighting. 

Then Russian forces captured a key port in Rotterdam and launched ballistic missile strikes on the south of the UK, London included, and the plan fell apart.

In the chaos the government relocated to Bletchley Park, and the streets around it were evacuated. The government has since moved on, and criminals and opportunists moved in.

## The developer

The Home Counties is made by Chris Rowles in Godot 4.7.

The current build is an early prototype: one map, one rifle you can build up at the gunsmith, three kinds of grenade, fourteen scavs (more in a squad), four traders and co-op for up to four.

New features are written up in the [dev log](../devlog/).
