## The game

The Home Counties is a single-player extraction shooter in the tradition of *Escape from Tarkov*, *Arena Breakout: Infinite* and *Road to Vostok*. You go into a raid with a loadout, search houses, crates and fallen scavs, fight armed scavs who use cover, flank and search for you, and need to reach an exit before the raid timer runs out. If you die or run out of time, you lose everything you were carrying.

## The story so far

Russia launched a full-scale invasion into Europe, and Britain became NATO's logistics hub. American and allied troops, vehicles, weapons and armour poured through its ports, airbases and depots, which is why US Army kit turns up in Bletchley.

Britain was already divided, worn down by COVID, heatwaves, cyberattacks and one political crisis after another. Russia saw a chance to cut the link between Europe and the US, and quietly funded criminal gangs and dissident groups, each with its own agenda. That's where the AKs came from.

The Home Counties, the ring of counties around London, were meant to be a protective barrier for the capital and a Civilian Protection and Continuity Zone for people displaced by forced requisitions, relocations and the fighting. Then Russian forces captured a key port in Rotterdam and launched ballistic missile strikes on the south of the UK, London included, and the plan fell apart.

In the chaos the government relocated to Bletchley Park, and the streets around it were evacuated. The government has since moved on. Criminals and opportunists moved in.

## The first map

The first map is called Duncombe Street, but it takes in more than one street: Osborne Street, Water Eaton Road, Oliver Road, Clifford Avenue, and the old Sainsbury's and its car park, with Victorian terraces by the West Coast Main Line and back gardens nobody has mown since the evacuation. It's built from real map data, so if you know Bletchley you'll recognise it. If you don't, the [tactical map](../map/) shows the layout.

The streets tell the rest of the story themselves, in the leaflets, window cards and soldiers' logs that were left behind.

## The developer

The Home Counties is made by Chris Rowles in Godot 4.

The current build is an early prototype with one map, one rifle and fourteen scavs. New features are written up in the [dev log](../devlog/).
