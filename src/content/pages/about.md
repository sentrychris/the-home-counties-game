## The game

The Home Counties is a single-player extraction shooter in the tradition of *Escape from Tarkov*, *Arena Breakout: Infinite* and *Road to Vostok*. You go into a raid with a loadout, loot crates and fallen scavs, fight armed scavs who use cover, flank and search for you, and need to reach an exit before the raid timer runs out. If you die or run out of time, you lose everything you were carrying.

It's set in a collapsed, overgrown Britain rather than a fictional war zone: Duncombe Street in Bletchley, Milton Keynes. The map has Victorian terraces by the West Coast Main Line, an abandoned Sainsbury's and its car park, wrecked British cars, and back gardens nobody has mown in years.

The street is built from real map data, so if you know Bletchley you'll recognise it. If you don't, the [tactical map](../map/) shows the layout.

## The developer

The Home Counties is made by Chris Rowles in Godot 4.

The current build is an early prototype with one map, one rifle and twelve scavs. New features are written up in the [dev log](../devlog/).
