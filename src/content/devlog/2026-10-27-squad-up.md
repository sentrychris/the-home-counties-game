## Playing together

The Home Counties is now a co-op game for up to four. The hideout's top bar has a **Squad** button: host a squad, or join one. Everyone keeps their own hideout, stash, cash and traders on their own PC, takes their own kit in and extracts into their own stash. Only the host can start the raid, once everyone's back in the hideout, and then you all go in together: the same map, the same start, the same weather and hour, standing side by side.

In the raid you see your squadmates as full bodies in their gear with their name over their head, and you hear their footsteps and shots. Each player's raid ends on its own when they extract, die or leave, and the others are told. There's no pause in a squad raid: Esc opens the menu over the raid, which carries on.

## Through Steam

The first version joined by address, which meant port forwarding and networking settings. The second goes through Steam. With Steam running, hosting makes a friends-only lobby and everything goes through Steam's relay, so friends can join from anywhere with nothing to set up and nothing extra to install. The Squad box lists your Steam friends who are in the game, with **Join** and **Invite** beside each, and a Steam invite or **Join Game** in the friends list works too.

The next builds add scavs, shared loot and the end of the raid. How to set up a squad is in the [Co-op squads](../../guides/co-op-squads/) guide.
