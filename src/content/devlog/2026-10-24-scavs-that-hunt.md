This build rewrites how scavs think. The pictures were taken with the developer overlay on, which shows what each scav is doing.

## Spotting

Scavs don't see you instantly any more. Their awareness builds the longer they can see or hear you, and when they're not sure, they get suspicious and walk over to look.

## Fighting

- They pick cover from the real shape of the street, weighing up how exposed it is and how far they'd have to run.
- They lean out round corners, so sometimes all you'll see is a head.
- They move up from cover to cover, and one lays down suppressing fire while another flanks round out of sight.
- They won't shoot through their mates.
- Badly hurt, a scav backs off once to patch itself up.

## Hunting

Duck out of sight and they don't just hold the angle. Scavs out in the open come after you, and the others go looking for a while, checking where you were heading and the corners nearby.

## Patrols and hearing

Scavs patrol and roam around the street, and when one finds a dead mate it crouches over the body for a few seconds, then searches the area. They hear you by how loud you are: grass is quieter underfoot than gravel, and gunfire carries a long way.

Scavs have a second voice now as well, so a group doesn't all sound like the same person.
