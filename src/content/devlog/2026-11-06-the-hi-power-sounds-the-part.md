## A new shot

The Hi-Power has a whole new set of sounds. Its shot is sharper and fuller, with its own short tail, and it sits a little under the AK, as a 9 mm should. Indoors it rings round the room. Other people's pistols are the same shot heard from where they are: close by, inside a building, duller down the street, and a pop far off beyond that, arriving late at the speed of sound.

## Drawing and reloading

Press <kbd>3</kbd> and the pistol comes out of its holster with its own sound, and you'll hear a scav's too when one runs dry and draws on you. The reload is three sounds, each started a moment early so its click lands with the hands: the release and the magazine sliding free as it drops, the new one clicking home as your hand pushes it up the grip, and after an empty magazine, the slide slamming home as your hand lets it go. Putting a pistol in its slot in the gear screen plays the slide as well.
