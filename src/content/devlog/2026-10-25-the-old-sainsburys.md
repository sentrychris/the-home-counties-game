The large building at the north-east end of the map was Sainsbury's in Bletchley, since demolished. In The Home Counties it's still standing, abandoned.

## The store

A long, low box with a level of black glass above, a white canopy over the entrance at the north end, big posters on white boards, and black bollards along the front. Its car park has white bays, blue trolley shelters, lamp posts, and birch and sycamore trees in the islands, which make handy cover.

## Roads

Every road, car park and path on the map is now laid with a scan of real worn tarmac: faded paint, oil stains, potholes, cracks, cracks sealed with bands of tar, and weeds coming through. There's more rubbish about too, with boxes, leaves in the gutters and junk in the road.

The colour grade was tried five different ways for this build. It stayed as it was: the fix was more detail, not new colours.
