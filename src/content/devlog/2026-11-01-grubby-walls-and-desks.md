## Lived in

The first insides looked too smooth, almost plastic. The walls are now painted plaster that's gone grubby, with darker patches where hands and furniture rubbed, water stains on the ceilings, and dirtier floors.

The bedrooms got real desks, two kinds, with an office chair tucked in where there's room for one. And the back doors moved along a little, so they don't open into the foot of the stairs.

## Around the street

- **Bagshot Court.** Where the two wings of the retirement flats meet, a few windows had brick right behind the glass. They've moved along so every window has a room behind it, and the back porch roof no longer cuts across the window above.
- **Manholes** sit just proud of the road, instead of sinking under the road paint and the grit.
- **Bin bags** no longer sit on doorsteps blocking the way in.

## Ticking things off

Finishing part of a task now plays a short sound, and finishing a whole task plays a bigger one. Both are Chris's own.

When a raid ends, the menu music comes back in on the end-of-raid screen and carries on into the hideout.

In the gear screen, the box with your tasks down the right is now always as tall as your gear beside it, and still scrolls when you have a lot on.
