With a street to move through, moving had to feel right.

## Movement

You can lean left and right with Q and E, vault and climb over walls and fences, and the view bobs as you walk and dips when you land. Scavs got proper animations to match: they walk with the rifle gripped properly, strafe, back off, crouch-walk, and fall when they die.

## Sound

The placeholder sounds went. The AK sounds like an AK, footsteps change with what's underfoot, and there's a background of wind, birds and distant noise. Scavs talk to each other too. A second sound pass later that night brought in a new scav voice, a punchier AK, body hits and the rattle of kit.

## Menus

The game got a front end: a title screen, a hideout with your loadout and stash and a choice of where to raid, a summary when you get out showing what you brought home and what it's worth, and a pause menu with settings and volume sliders.
