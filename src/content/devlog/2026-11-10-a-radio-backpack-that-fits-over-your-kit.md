## The radio backpack

The radio backpack is a camouflage pack with an army radio set strapped on top, aerial, handset and all. The battery died long ago, but the pack still carries well.

It takes 4x5 squares in your stash and holds a 5x5 grid, a little less than the military rucksack's 5x6. At 4 kg empty it's the heaviest pack, as the set comes with it, and it's worth about £800. Chalky sells it from his third level, and about one scav in fifty carries one.

## Fitted over whatever you wear

Like the helmet, plate carrier and chest rig, the pack is fitted to the body rather than stuck on its back. Its shoulder straps are remade to lie along the body, and the buckles are lifted off wherever they would dig in. What's under it matters too: the straps lie differently over a hoodie, a shoulder holster, a chest rig or a plate carrier. So there are eight fittings, one for each mix of those, and the game picks the one for what you're wearing.

The shoulder holster now bends with your back as well, with or without a backpack. It used to be stiff, and with the radio backpack on, the pack's straps cut up to 8 cm into its pouches as you walked. Now the holster and the pack move together, and walking the worst is about 2 mm.

Every fitting was checked in every animation: walking, running, crouching, aiming, firing and reloading. Walking and running, the straps now stay within a few millimetres in every fitting. Over a plate carrier, crouching or aiming folds the carrier's lower magazine pouches over the strap webbing, so it looks tucked in under them.

The pictures are cloud renders of a scav in the new pack.
