## Arms

You can see your own arms holding the rifle now, and Caps Lock gives you a slow, quiet walk.

## Grass

The flat grass texture was replaced with real blades close up: thick tufts growing up against walls, kerbs and fences, and long grass across the open ground. Further off, the grass fades into a cheaper picture of the same tufts, so a field doesn't cost too much to draw.

## Wrecked British cars

The cars in the road are now real British models, every one of them wrecked or left to rot: Vauxhall Corsas, Ford Fiestas, a Vauxhall Cavalier and Jaguar XJs, plus scans of burnt-out shells. Some are on bricks, some have smashed glass, and the paint is faded and filthy.

## Gritty

The picture has a harder, grittier look now, closer to Tarkov and Arena Breakout. It's the default, but each effect can be switched off under Settings > Picture, and the old Natural look is still there if you prefer it.
