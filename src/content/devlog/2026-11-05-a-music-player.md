## Five tracks

Until now the hideout had one piece of music, 1026, going round and round since the loading screen went in. Now there are five: 1026, then four new ones, Посылка пришла, Unexpected Item In The Bagging Area, Watching It All and Compulsory Purchase. They play one after another, and they're levelled so none of them comes in louder than the rest.

## The player

On the right of the bottom bar, beside the build number: small bars that move with the music, the track's name (a long one slides along), a thin line for how far in it is, and buttons for shuffle, previous, play and stop, next, and the track list. Shuffle plays every track once before any comes round again. Previous goes back to the start of the track if it's more than a few seconds in.

## The track list

The list button, or a click on the track's name, opens the list above the bar: every track with its length, the one playing marked. Click a track to play it, click or drag along the line underneath to skip through it, and set the music's volume there too (it's the same as the Music slider in Settings). Click anywhere else, or press Esc, to close it.

## Stop means stop

Stop the music and it stays off: on the title screen, in the hideout and after a raid, until you press play again, and then it carries on from where it stopped. The track, shuffle and stop are remembered for next time. As before, the music fades out on the loading screen and never plays in a raid.
