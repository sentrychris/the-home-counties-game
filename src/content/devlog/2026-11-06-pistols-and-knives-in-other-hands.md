## The pistol in both hands

Until now, anyone you saw with the Hi-Power or the Ka-Bar out still moved as if they had a rifle. Now they have animations of their own. The pistol comes up in both hands, standing or down on one knee, and stays up as they walk and run in any direction, their feet keeping pace with the ground. Flat out they sprint with it held in close, and bring it up again when they stop. Each shot flips the muzzle up before it settles back, and a scav turns its shoulders to keep it on you.

## The knife

The knife is held low and close on guard, blade down, and the stab is a hard blow from overhead. A scav's stab now lands when the blade comes down, about half a second after its arm goes up, so if you see it raised you have a moment to get out of reach.

## In a squad

Your friends see you the same way. When you draw your pistol or knife, your body holds it in these poses, and when you stab, they see the blow.
