## Things left behind

The street's first set of papers told you what the council and the army said. This second set is about the people. One house on Duncombe Street was never left: someone stayed when everyone else got on the trains, and their things are still where they put them, down to a wind-up radio on the windowsill and the notepad beside it. Elsewhere there's a mum's letter to Jamie, notes left for neighbours and for whoever came next, a weapons handover sheet in an army tent, and a newspaper in a hallway. Two or three of the short notes turn up each raid, so a house is worth a second look.

## Keeping them

Tap **F** to read a paper where it lies, or hold **F** to take it. Bring it home and it's pinned to a new **PAPERS** board in the hideout, for good, so you can piece the street's story together over many raids. In a squad, everyone takes their own copy.
