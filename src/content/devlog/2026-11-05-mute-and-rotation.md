## Mute

The speaker beside the volume slider in the track list is now a mute button. The music carries on silently, so it's where you left it when you unmute, and the bottom bar shows a crossed-out speaker in place of the moving bars. Moving the volume, in the list or in Settings, brings the music back.

## Your own rotation

Every track in the list has a tick at the end of its row. Untick one and it's left out: next, shuffle and the end of a track skip over it, and it's greyed out in the list. You can still click it to play it whenever you like. The list's header counts how many tracks are in the rotation and how long they run, and at least one always stays in. The game remembers your choices for next time.

## The Home Counties

The first track, the one that's played on the title screen and in the hideout since the loading screen went in, now has a proper name: The Home Counties.

## Hideout and Raid

The tabs on the bottom bar now read HIDEOUT and RAID, in that order, the same as along the top bar. HIDEOUT used to say STORAGE; it's the same page, with your gear and your stash.
