## F12

<kbd>F12</kbd> hides everything on screen in a raid: the compass, your body figure, the task box and the notices. Press it again to bring them back. Like every key, you can change it in Settings > Controls.

## Photo mode

When you're playing on your own, the Esc menu has a **Photo mode** button under Resume. The raid stops behind it: nothing moves, and no scav sees you. It isn't there in a squad, because a squad's raid never stops.

- **The camera.** A free camera that starts just behind your shoulder, so you can see yourself and your kit. Hold the right mouse button to look round, move with your movement keys, go up with <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>E</kbd> and down with <kbd>C</kbd> or <kbd>Q</kbd>, <kbd>Shift</kbd> for faster and <kbd>Alt</kbd> for slower. It goes up to 20 metres from you, and never through walls. Or pick **Your eyes** for your own view, rifle and all.
- **The lens.** Field of view and tilt, and depth of field: click the picture to focus on what's there, and open up the blur.
- **The look.** Six colour filters (the game's own, black and white, sepia, warm, cold and faded film), exposure, contrast and saturation, a vignette and film grain.
- **The frame.** A rule of thirds, golden ratio or centre cross grid, and letterboxes for 2.39:1, 1.85:1, 4:3, square and 4:5 portrait.
- **The world.** Show yourself or hide, and change the weather and the time of day, night included.

<kbd>P</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd> takes the photo, without the panel or the grid and cut to the letterbox, and saves it as a PNG in Pictures\The Home Counties. <kbd>H</kbd> or <kbd>F12</kbd> hides the panel, <kbd>R</kbd> puts the camera back, and Esc returns to the menu with everything as you left it.
