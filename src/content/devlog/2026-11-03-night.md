## 23:00

Night has arrived on Duncombe Street, though not in the normal rotation yet. For now you choose it from the time of day switcher in the developer menu, while it's being tuned.

A full moon hangs low in the south, there are stars, and the sky is a deep grey. On a clear night the street is properly dark: you can make out shapes, and not much more. Mist lifts it a little, and overcast and rain are darker still.

Indoors there's nothing coming through the windows, so the houses and the old Sainsbury's are pitch black unless something's burning. Fires, the camp's glow and the scavs' work lamps still do. This is what the [weapon lights](../2026-11-03-weapon-lights/) are for.

The birds go quiet, and now and then a tawny owl calls somewhere off in the trees.

## Scavs in the dark

Scavs can't see as far at night: a bit over half as far on a clear night, less in cloud, rain or mist. Unless your light is on, or you've just fired without a suppressor. Then the muzzle flash or the beam gives you away, and they see you as far off as they would by day.

## No crosshair by default

The crosshair is now off unless you turn it on, under Settings > Game. Aim with your sights.
