## Both hands on the grip

Some of the pistol's animations held the left hand open beside the gun, as if it were still round a rifle's handguard: kneeling, walking, strafing, backing off and sprinting. Now the left hand always wraps round the right on the grip, whatever the body is doing, and only lets go to throw a grenade. That goes for scavs, your squadmates, and you as other players see you.

## The knife on guard

With the knife out and nobody in reach, they stand on guard: knees bent, the knife low at the right hip with the blade down, and the other hand out in front. The overhead swing only comes when they stab, and you have about half a second to get back out of reach.
