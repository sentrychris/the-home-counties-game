## No more flicker

The grit round the blast marks used to flicker on and off as you walked past. The burn, the grit, the puddles, the leaves and the wear on the roads are all thin see-through layers lying on the tarmac, and where two of them overlapped, the game picked which to draw on top by how far each one was from you. Walk a few steps and the answer changed, so the grit kept vanishing under the burn and coming back. Every layer on the ground and the walls now has a fixed place in the stack: the wear on the road at the bottom, then the blast, its burn, moss, stains, silt, puddles and leaves, with the grit on top.

## Thinner dust

The grit's film of dust was a smooth pale disc, and from close by it looked more like a puddle than debris. It's now about a third as thick, patchy and with a ragged edge, so what you see is the broken brick and concrete itself.
