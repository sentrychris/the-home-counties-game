## Tasks

The traders have always set small jobs. Now they have proper tasks as well: 36 of them, nine from each trader, in three stages. Each trader gives you three to start with. When you've done those and they trust you enough, the next three open up, and so on.

Some are about finding something and bringing it home. Some send you to have a look at a place and report back, or to carry something across the map to where it's needed. Some are about the scavs, and ask for it done a certain way. A few have to be done in one raid. What each one is about, and what the traders make of what you find, is for you to find out in the game.

## Keeping track

There's a Tasks tab in the hideout now, with what each trader has for you, what you've done and what's still locked. In a raid, your current tasks are on the gear screen and the pause menu, and a small box slides in at the top right as you tick things off: OBJECTIVE COMPLETE for each step, MISSION COMPLETE when you've done the lot.

## Dead Air

The first task Chalky gives you is Dead Air. Someone has been broadcasting the same message on an old emergency frequency every evening, and Chalky wants to know who's still talking out there. You'll need a wind-up radio.

The voice on the radio is a real recorded reading, put through the radio's speaker and the drop-outs of a weak signal. Stand close and listen to the end.
