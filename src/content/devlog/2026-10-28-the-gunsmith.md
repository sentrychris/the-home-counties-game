## A bench for your rifle

The hideout has a new **GUNSMITH** tab (or right-click a rifle > Modify at the gunsmith). Your rifles are listed down the left, wherever they are, and the one you pick sits big in the middle on its own lit bench. Drag to turn it, right-drag to move it and scroll to zoom. A card on a thin line points to each slot: **Sight, Mount, Muzzle, Stock** and **Magazine**.

Pick a slot and its parts are listed with what they do. Point at one to see it on the rifle and the stats change, green for better and red for worse. Nothing is final until you press **Assemble**: parts come from your stash first, then your bag, and anything you don't have is bought from Carl, after asking. Parts that come off go back in the stash.

## Parts

- **AK side rail mount**, which any sight needs.
- **4x prism scope**, with a lit red chevron.
- **7.62 compact suppressor**: no muzzle flash, and heard at under a third of the distance.
- **The wooden stock**, now a part of its own. Take it off and the rifle's lighter and quicker to raise, but it kicks a third more.

The stats are the ones Arena Breakout uses: ergonomics, vertical and horizontal recoil control, accuracy, muzzle velocity, effective range, fire rate and weight. They change how the rifle really handles in a raid, and the AK as it comes shoots exactly as it did before. Every number is in the [Gunsmith](../../guides/gunsmith/) guide.
