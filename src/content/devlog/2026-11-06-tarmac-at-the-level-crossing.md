## The last of the old slabs

When the pavements went over to tarmac in v0.1.179, four short ramps were missed: the ones that lift the pavements up onto the level crossing on Water Eaton Road, two on each side of the line. They were still the old pale square slabs, so a light patch stood out at every corner of the crossing. They're tarmac now, the same as the pavement they rise from, so nothing changes as you walk up onto the crossing.

## The kerb runs up to the deck

The kerb used to stop short of each ramp, leaving a strip of bare ground between the road and the pavement. Now it carries on all the way: kerbstones rise up beside the road to the rubber deck over the rails. On two of the corners the road is a little wider than the crossing, so the last stones bend gently in to meet it, with tarmac filled in behind them.
