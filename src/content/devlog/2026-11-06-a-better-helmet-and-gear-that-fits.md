## A new MICH

The old MICH helmet was a scan of a helmet on a mannequin's head, cut off at the brim, which left a ragged edge, and it perched on top of the head. The new one is a proper model of a high-cut helmet in ranger green: side rails, a night-vision shroud on the front, velcro patches, and an H-harness with a chin cup. It's sized to the head, 22 cm across, and sits 6 cm lower than before, with about two centimetres between the shell and the head all round (about the thickness of its pads) and the brim just over the eyebrows. The straps run down the cheeks to a cup under the chin.

## Gear that fits

The helmet, the plate carrier and the chest rig used to sit stiffly on one bone each, so they floated in places and cut into the body in others: the carrier's shoulder straps hovered a few centimetres over the shoulders, its sides sank into the hoodie under the arms, and the rig's back straps were buried inside the back. Now each is fitted to the body: wrapped onto the hoodie and the head so its inside sits just off the cloth, then tied to the same bones as the clothes. The straps lie on the shoulders and follow them, nothing pokes through, and it all bends with the body when a scav, a squadmate or you aim, crouch and walk. A chest rig worn over a plate carrier now sits on the carrier's front plate in place of its magazine pouches, like a placard, instead of disappearing inside it.
