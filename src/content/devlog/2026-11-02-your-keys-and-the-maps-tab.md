## Settings > Controls

Settings has a new **Controls** page with every control on it, in three columns: movement, weapons, and gear and the world. Click a key, then press the new one, or a mouse button. Each control can have two keys, and Alt, Ctrl or Shift with a key works too.

If the key you pick is already used, the two controls swap, and a note tells you so. Right-click a key to clear it, and **Reset to defaults** puts everything back. Esc stays as it is, for backing out of menus.

## Raid comes second

The top bar now reads Hideout, Raid, Character, Traders, Gunsmith, Papers, Maps and Tasks, so the raid is one click from home.

## Maps

The **Maps** tab lists the maps: Duncombe Street, the practice yard, and two marked coming soon, The Station in Bletchley and Central MK. Open one to see its full tactical map: scroll to zoom, drag to move, double-click to look closer.

The map has been redrawn from the street as it is now, not as it was a few weeks ago: the real hoarding round the raid, the army camp and tents behind Bagshot Court, the inside of the old Sainsbury's, 14 scavs and 24 crates. The [tactical map](../../map/) on this site has been redrawn to match.
