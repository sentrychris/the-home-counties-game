Exits need to be easy to find, so this build marks them clearly.

## Checkpoints

There are two exits from Duncombe Street: **Station Footpath** in the north-west corner, and **Oliver Road** in the north-east, by the old Sainsbury's. Both are extraction zones with a red smoke flare, so you can spot them over the rooftops.

To extract, stay in the green zone for ten seconds. If the raid timer reaches zero first, you lose everything you were carrying.

## The field copy

Duncombe Street also has a tactical map now, drawn from the game's own map on a 25-metre grid. It shows both start points, both exits, the twelve scav spawns, seventeen crate spots and every house with an open door.

You can [explore it on the site](../../map/) and switch its layers on and off.

> From the field copy: watch the railway, you can hear movement for miles.
