## The magazine actually comes out

Until now a scav's reload was a stock animation. The magazine never left the rifle, and the move lifted the rifle up so high that the magazine went straight through the scav's jaw.

Now a reload is three seconds of real work. The scav brings the rifle in low in front of his chest, tilted so the magazine faces his left hand. He rocks the empty magazine forward off its catch, pulls it out and drops it in a pocket on his left hip. Then he takes a full one from the pouch on the front of his rig, hooks the front of it into the rifle, rocks it back till it clicks, and reaches over the top to rack the bolt. He glances down at the rifle while he does it, and every step makes its own noise, so you can hear him reloading round a corner.

## Squadmates and your own body too

Your squadmates change magazines the same way, for as long as their reload really takes. A reload from the backpack is slower, so their hand stays in the pouch for longer. Your own body does it too, in photo mode and in your shadow. In a squad raid your friends see the host's scavs reload the same way.
