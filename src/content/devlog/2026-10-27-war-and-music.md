## The far-off war

There's always been fighting somewhere beyond the Zone, heard as distant gunfire and booms. This round made it sound like it's really out there. Indoors, it's heard through the walls: muffled, low and echoing, as it is inside the old supermarket, and a big boom rattles the windows. The night crickets are gone and the birds and wind are quieter, so the war carries.

A heavier rework with jets and helicopters followed, but the original far-off war simply sounded better, so it came back a day later, keeping the indoor muffling and the rattling windows.

## Music and loading

The title screen and hideout now have music, with its own volume slider in Settings. Starting a raid brings up a loading screen over the title's backdrop: a thin bar along the bottom that shows real progress, a spinning ring, and one of 42 tips beside it, all of them British. While it loads, the music fades into the sound of the far-off war, so you arrive already listening for it.

Loading itself got a lot quicker on the way: about 29 seconds with a frozen screen down to about 14 with everything moving.
