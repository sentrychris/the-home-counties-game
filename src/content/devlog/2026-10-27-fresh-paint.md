## Graffiti

The old cartoon graffiti is gone. In its place are fourteen original pieces made for the game: eight full pieces (VEKS, SKEMA, FENZ, ORKA, DUSK, ZEPH, ROKIT and OMEN), three throw-ups and three walls of tags. Each one is painted letter by letter with outlines, 3D, fades, shines and drips, then sun-faded, flaked and dirtied so it looks like it's been there a while. They're sprayed on as decals at 55 spots along the north hoarding and round the old supermarket, so the board or brick still shows through the paint.

## Fixes

The first play of the new houses turned up a few things, all fixed here:

- An open door's glass now swings with the door instead of staying in the frame.
- Bay windows are no longer cut off at the corners.
- A black cross on a corner house's side wall is gone.
- 44 fences across the map were cut back or taken out where they ran across doors and paths.
- Two houses by the Station footpath no longer overlap.
- The army tents sit square to the car park bays.
