## The rifle

The AK-47 is now a detailed, properly worn model. Its magazine, bolt and trigger are separate moving parts, so the bolt kicks back when you fire and the magazine really comes out when you reload.

## The arms

New first-person arms hold it, in black knuckle gloves and camo sleeves, with animations for aiming, firing, sprinting and reloading. You see the whole reload: old magazine out, new one in, then a pull on the bolt.

## A second AK

Settings > Weapon lets you pick your rifle: the worn AK, or a cleaner AK in wood and steel, woodland, desert or winter camo. Scavs keep the worn one.

## Field of view

A field of view setting went in with a new, Tarkov-style rifle position, and Settings is now split into tabs.
