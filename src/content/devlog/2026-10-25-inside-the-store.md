The old Sainsbury's isn't just a backdrop any more. You can go in.

## Ways in

The front shutter is stuck about a metre up, so you crawl underneath. Round the back there's a fire door, and the goods-in shutter is open.

## Inside

Twelve aisles of stripped shelving, some of it toppled, checkouts, chillers, a pharmacy counter, and a frozen aisle under a fallen-in roof. Daylight comes in through the rooflights and the holes, and deeper in it's dark. Behind it all is the warehouse with its racking.

## The scav camp

Scavs have set up camp among the shelves, lit by a pair of work lights. There are four crates in the store and two scavs inside, which brings Duncombe Street to fourteen scavs in all.
