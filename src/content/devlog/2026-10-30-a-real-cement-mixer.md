## Out with the orange

Both building sites had a cement mixer that was put together from simple shapes in code, painted bright orange. It's been replaced with a photo-scanned mixer: grey, worn and spattered with old cement, standing where the builders left it.

The scan was far too detailed to use as it came, so it was thinned down until it looked the same from a step away and cost almost nothing to draw. The same tools can now do that for any scanned prop.
