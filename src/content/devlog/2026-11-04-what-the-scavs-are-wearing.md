## Everyone in white

Until now every scav on Duncombe Street wore the same white tracksuit with black stripes and the same black trainers. That made them easy to spot, and it made a group of them look like a team.

## What they wear now

Each scav's clothes are rolled as it turns up:

- **Tracksuits**, about three in ten, in nine colourways with their stripes, some with ordinary trousers instead of the bottoms.
- **Everyday clothes**, about four in ten: a hoodie over jeans, joggers, chinos, work trousers or cords.
- **Army surplus**, the rest: old British DPM, MTP, desert DPM, olive drab, black or coyote, sometimes matching top and bottom. A scav in a plate carrier or a helmet is more likely to dress the part.

Then trainers or boots, and dirt and fading, so nothing looks new. Without a helmet, nearly half of them wear a knitted beanie, and about a third are clean-shaven. It comes to well over a thousand different looks.

## Telling friend from foe

You and your squad wear near-black tracksuits, so no scav is dark from top to bottom: a black or navy tracksuit always has its white stripes. In a squad, everyone sees the same clothes on the same scav.
