## A new first track

The music player in the hideout's bottom bar now starts with **Зима** (Russian for "winter"), a new track that opens with a folk choir. After it come Посылка пришла, Watching It All and Compulsory Purchase, levelled like the others so none of them comes in louder than the rest.

## Two tracks off the list

The Home Counties, the old menu music, and Unexpected Item In The Bagging Area are no longer on the list, so the player has four tracks, about 14 minutes in all.

If you were last listening to one of the tracks that has gone, the music picks up with Зима the next time you start the game. If you had unticked one of them to leave it out of the rotation, that tick is forgotten. Everything else you set stays as it was: shuffle, stop, mute and any other tracks you left out.
