## Shoot the glass

Every pane of glass on the map can break: each light of every house window and door, the Brunel Centre's curtain wall, the old supermarket's black glass, and every car's windscreen and windows. A pane might hold the first round, leaving a small hole with a frosted ring and cracks running out from it, but the third always takes it out.

## How it breaks

A smashed pane breaks along its own cracks. Teeth of glass stay in the frame, glinting at their edges, while the pieces in between slide out one after another with a spray of small bits, tumble, bounce and lie flat where they land, for good, on a glitter of broken glass. Car windscreens are laminated, so they hold however often they're hit. Side windows craze into little cubes and drop out a moment later. The crash and the tinkle carry 120 metres. A notice pinned over a door's glass flutters down with it and can still be read where it lands.

## Shooting through

You can shoot through a window into a room, or through a car, and so can the scavs. A frag within a few metres blows windows in. In a squad the host decides what breaks, and someone who joins late still sees what's already broken. None of it costs anything extra to draw until it breaks.
