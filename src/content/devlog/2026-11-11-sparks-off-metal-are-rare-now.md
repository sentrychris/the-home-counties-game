## Why

Since v0.1.216 every round that hit a lamp post threw a shower of sparks, and every one that hit a car about half as many. Real rounds hardly ever do that. A round with a lead core doesn't spark off steel at all, and even rounds with steel in their cores, like the AK's, only now and then strike sparks off hard steel, mostly when they glance off it. Brick and concrete don't spark.

## What you see now

- **Thick steel** (lamp posts, bollards, the railway's rails and masts): sparks from about one round in 50 that hits it square on, up to about one in 17 when the round only grazes it. When they come, the shower is smaller than before, with the same quick flash.
- **Thinner metal** (cars, bins, corrugated sheets, sign posts, railings): about one round in 200, up to one in 70 grazing.
- **Brick, concrete, stone and gravel:** never.

The rest of the time a round hitting metal does what it really does: a grey puff of lead and the mark it leaves, a splash of lead on thick steel or a punched hole in thinner metal, with the clang or ring you hear. The puff is a little bigger than before, so you can still see where it hit.

The sounds are the same as in v0.1.216, so a clang off the lamp post next to you still tells you someone has your line.
