## Aim and gear

Scav rifles used to point off to one side when they fired at you, a leftover from the animations. Now the scav's upper body turns so the barrel lines up with where it's aiming. Their backpacks are closed underneath, and the black K6-3 helmet sits properly on the head with its visor slot at eye level.

## Side vision

A scav could walk right past you if you stood still beside it. Now they notice you close by out of the corner of their eye. Stand still three metres to one side and a scav looks round in about half a second, and right behind one at arm's length, it'll turn within a couple of seconds. Sixteen metres to the side, you're still fine.

## Fewer join a fight

One gunfight used to bring four to ten scavs from all over the street. Now gunfire brings scavs looking from within 60 metres, and at most three come to a fight they only heard. The rest stand guard where they are, often crouched, for a while.
