Testing a looter takes a lot of looting, so the prototype has developer menus. They're for testing and will come out before any release.

## In a raid

F1 opens a slim panel down the right in three columns. **You:** god mode, don't die, infinite stamina, infinite ammo, fly, heal fully, refill ammo. **Scavs:** freeze them, stop them seeing or hearing you, show what each one is up to, spawn one in front of you, kill them all. **Raid and world:** stop the timer, slow motion, add five minutes, set the weather and hour, start a far-off fight. An Items page drops anything in the game at your feet, and a Recoil slider (added later) scales the AK's kick from nothing to double.

## In the hideout

F1 in the hideout sets your cash, steps any trader through their four levels, restocks every trader or fills every shelf, hands in every job, and passes a raid without playing one. Click any item to put it in your stash, or Shift+click to put it straight on you.
