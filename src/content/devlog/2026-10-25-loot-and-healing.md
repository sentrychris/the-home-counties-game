## Real items

First aid kits, bandages, bottles of water, an old phone, a gold watch, painkillers and a surgical kit all have proper models now, and that's what you see when you drop them. Dropped things lie flat on the road or pavement instead of standing on end or sinking out of sight, and you can walk straight through them.

## Healing takes time

You can't shoot while you heal. Your rifle goes down, you move at half speed, and a bar under the crosshair shows how long is left. Firing, or switching to a rifle, stops it.

| Item | Takes | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bandage | 3 seconds | Heals 50 on a hurt body part that isn't bleeding or destroyed. One use. |
| First aid kit | 3 seconds a part | Heals from a supply of 400 points. |
| Surgical kit | 10 seconds | Stops bleeding, or mends a destroyed part. Three uses. |
| Painkillers | 2 seconds | For 90 seconds: no head blur, hurt limbs work, and hits do a little less damage. |

Food and water don't heal you.

Right-click an item and choose **Use on** to pick the body part, or press H and the game picks the most sensible treatment.

> The surgical kit is the only thing that stops bleeding, and scavs make you bleed. Carry one.
