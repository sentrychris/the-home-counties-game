Most of the Duncombe Street map isn't road. It's back gardens: long, narrow strips between fences, overgrown after a few years without anyone looking after them.

This build fills them in with photoreal trees and hedges, knee-high grass, garden furniture, and close-boarded fences that block your view.

## How it changes raids

From the road you can see a long way. In the gardens you often can't see past the next fence. That makes them good for moving unseen, and risky when a scav is on the other side of a shed.

> **Tip:** long grass hides you a little, but it doesn't make you quieter. Scavs listen as well as look.
