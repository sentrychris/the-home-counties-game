## The cottages

The row of cottages on the north side of Osborne Street, between Oliver Road and the checkpoint, used to be eight copies of the same cream house. They've been rebuilt one by one from photos of the real street, in the order they stand: a gabled red-brick pair, a white-rendered house, two cottages sharing a recessed porch under a date stone, one with a buff brick bay, and a white gabled pair by the checkpoint.

Each has its own brick, render, bays, porches, sills and chimneys, the way the real ones do. The white houses were then repainted after the first test pictures, when they came out looking grey.

## The back fences

Behind Duncombe Street, a few back-garden fences had gone wrong: one ran off diagonally into the field, two crossed each other, and one garden was squeezed down to a strip a metre wide. A couple of lone houses out the back were planning their gardens first and carving up the neighbours'. The gardens are now laid out the right way round, and the stray fences and a leftover wall in the field are gone.
