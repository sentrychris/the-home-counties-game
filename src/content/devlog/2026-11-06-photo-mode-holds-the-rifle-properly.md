## The rifle left behind

From your own eyes, your body stands a little behind the camera so you never see inside your own head. Photo mode's Show yourself steps it forward to where your squad sees you, but the rifle isn't part of the body, and with the game paused nothing put it back in your hands. It stayed where it was: 8 cm back when standing, 30 cm back when kneeling. So in photo mode your front hand sat on the barrel, and kneeling it was out at the muzzle, like holding a rocket launcher.

Now the rifle, pistol and knife move with your body as it steps into view. Your right hand is on the pistol grip, your left on the handguard, and when you aim your cheek is on the stock.

Your squadmates were never affected: in co-op, friends always saw you holding the rifle properly.
