## The checkpoint, upgraded

The checkpoint at the east end of Osborne Street was first put together in an afternoon. It's now been done properly. Every sign was redrawn as a weathered, real-looking army sign, and the HOME COUNTIES ZONE sign over the gate is much bigger and better made.

Beside it, on Bagshot Court's side, is an army camp: an HQ tent and a bunk tent you can walk into, with camp beds, tables and chairs, the light coming soft through the canvas. Getting the inside of a tent to look right took most of a night; the first tries were pitch black in there.

## Every item makes a sound

Guns, gear and magazines already made sounds as you moved them, but medical kit and everything else was silent. Now every item makes its own sound as you pick it up and put it down: the crackle of a blister pack, the crinkle of a wrapper, a canvas pouch, a plastic case, a slosh of water, a tin, a jingle, the clank of a toolbox, the dull thud of a car battery.

## And a fix

A raid could now and then get stuck at 15 FPS after loading. It no longer does.
