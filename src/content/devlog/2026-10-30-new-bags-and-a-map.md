## New bags

The two backpacks have been replaced. The small one is now a **Black daypack**, the kind every commuter had, and the big one a **Military rucksack** in British Army camouflage. Both were toned down to sit in with the rest of the kit, and both now sit snug against your back instead of floating behind it. They're on the [gear page](../../gear/).

## A map on paper

The map on the Raid tab was last drawn weeks ago, before the pub, the army camp, the Clifford Avenue site and half of what's in the street now. It's been redrawn from the current map, on a sunny afternoon so it reads clearly.

It's printed on aged map paper now: foxed and creased where it's been folded, with a proper border, a scale band and grid numbers round the edge, and the corners taped down. There are a few new notes in the same hand, and the paper edges of the taped note beside it are smooth instead of jagged.
