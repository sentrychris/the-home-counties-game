## Weather and time of day

Each raid rolls its weather: sunny spells (about a third of raids), overcast, rain, or morning mist. It rolls the hour too: early morning, midday, or late evening, when the low sun turns everything gold. Mist only comes early in the morning.

## Rain

Rain is drawn with streaks from Columbia University's rain streak database, made from a physical model of how real raindrops fall and catch the light, so it looks like rain rather than lines. The street gets wet, and the puddles reflect the sky.

## Lighting fixes

Some windows used to look black, as if every house had blackout curtains. The rooms behind them are now lit by the daylight. Looking towards the sun is less hazy, the shadows are deeper, and the terraces no longer look washed out.

## Display settings

There's a new Display tab in Settings: fullscreen, borderless or windowed, any resolution, and FSR 2 upscaling (Quality, Balanced or Performance) for more frames.
