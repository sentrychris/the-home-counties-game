## A second rifle

The M4A1 is the game's second rifle, alongside the AK-47. It goes in either rifle slot, takes 5x2 squares in your stash, and comes with its carry handle (the iron sights: a peep sight at the back, a post at the front) and an A2 birdcage on the muzzle. As it comes it's worth about £1,700, against the AK's £1,200.

It's the handier of the two. It's lighter (3.1 kg as it comes, against 3.5), quicker to raise and aim, kicks less both up and to the side, is more accurate, fires faster (800 rounds a minute against 600) and reloads a little quicker. Its rounds hit a little softer than the AK's, but get through armour a little better.

## Rounds and magazines

The M4 shoots 5.56x45 from 30-round STANAG magazines: black polymer, light, and like the AK's, too long for a 1x1 pocket. An AK magazine won't fit it, and a STANAG won't fit the AK. There are three kinds of round, as for the AK: FMJ, armour-piercing and hollow points, each loaded into a magazine the same way.

## At the gunsmith

The M4 has its own slots. The muzzle takes the birdcage or a 5.56 suppressor. The sight is always something: the carry handle, a tube red dot on its own riser, or the AK's open red dot or 4x scope, straight on the M4's top rail with no mount needed. The scout and compact lights go on the rail along the left of the handguard, and a vertical foregrip on the bottom rail takes out more of the sideways kick. Your left hand stays on the handguard behind it, using it as a stop.

## In your hands

The M4 uses the same arm animations as the AK, with each hand moved onto the M4's own grip and handguard. The rear sight was lined up so the front post sits in the middle of its ring when you aim, and the tube red dot sits at the height of the irons, so its dot lands on the front post. Reloading, the magazine comes straight down out of the well instead of rocking out like the AK's, and the dust cover is open while the bolt's back.

## How it sounds

The M4 has its own shot, suppressed shot, magazine out and in, and bolt. Other people's M4s sound like M4s too, from close by, down the street and far off. The echoes off the street, the ring of a room indoors and the far-off reflections are the AK's, as there's no 5.56 set of those yet.

## Where to find one

Carl sells M4s from his second level, with STANAG magazines, 5.56 rounds and the M4's parts; the suppressor comes later. He buys them back, and wants 5.56 AP. About one scav in eight carries an M4, built up now and then like the AK, and crates turn up 5.56 rounds and STANAG magazines, less often than the AK's.
