## Work stopped

A gap on the east side of Clifford Avenue is now a housing site where work stopped and never started again, built from a piece of concept art. Behind a fence of mesh panels and boards are the foundations of a small house, laid properly brick by brick as cavity walls a few courses high, set on hardcore with weeds pushing through. Around it: pallets of blocks and bags of cement, a skip, a portaloo, pipes and rubble. One fence panel has come down, and that's the way in.

The site uses real scanned models of building materials and site gear, and the building site by the north wall got the same ones, so both look the part.

## Something to find

A build later the site got a loot crate, tucked inside the foundations where the kitchen would have been. The whole site costs about half a millisecond on the test PC.
