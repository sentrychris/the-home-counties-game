## Holding it like a soldier would

v0.1.207 put your left hand on the M4's vertical foregrip, but on someone else, a scav, a squadmate or the figure on the end-of-raid screen, it didn't look like a hand holding anything. It sat behind the grip with its knuckles about 4 cm in front of it, so the fingers had to bend further than real fingers can to get round, and close up the hand looked crumpled.

Now it closes on the grip the way a real hand does. The grip lies across the palm, from the index knuckle to the heel of the hand, right up under the handguard. The fingers lie side by side and close round its front, each joint bent only as far as a finger bends. The thumb comes round the back and lies across the fingers on the other side, as in photos of soldiers holding one. Through a reload the hand still goes to the magazine and back, opening as it leaves the grip and closing again as it takes hold.

Your own first-person hand is the gloved one from v0.1.207, unchanged.

The pictures are cloud renders of the end-of-raid figure, with no map around it.
