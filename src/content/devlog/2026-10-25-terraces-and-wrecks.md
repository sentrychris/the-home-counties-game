Duncombe Street now has its Victorian terraces running down both sides: bay windows, chimneys with aerials, double yellow lines and boarded-up front doors.

## Wrecked British cars

Abandoned cars are scattered along the street: hatchbacks with smashed windscreens, a burnt-out 4x4, and a police estate by the checkpoint. They work as cover and as landmarks.

## Scavs

Armed scavs are now in the game. They spawn at twelve fixed points around Duncombe Street. They take cover when shot at, try to flank, and come to investigate noises.

These screenshots were taken with developer mode on, which is why "god mode" appears in some of them.

> Most crates are inside the open houses, so expect to run into scavs there too.
