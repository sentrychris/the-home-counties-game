## Who they are

The hideout has a Traders tab now. There are four of them, and you never see any of them in person: just an ID card, their prices, the notes they leave and the jobs they want doing.

- **Chalky, the Fixer.** Armour, helmets, rigs and packs. Ex-army logistics who kept the paperwork. Holds little, knows where everything is, and takes orders.
- **Carl, the Former Marine.** Rifles, pistols, rounds and blades. Every gun he sells, he's fired.
- **Crapper, the Scavenger.** Junk, electronics, valuables and cider. Takes almost anything off you.
- **Sofie, the Doctor.** Medical kit, water and food, and a tab once she trusts you.

## How trading works

You start with £1,000, and cash is never lost in a raid. Each trader pays a share of what a thing is worth, depending on how much they want that kind of thing, and two wanted items each pay half as much again. Sell them too much of one thing and they're full up for a while. They restock after every raid.

Trading with someone and doing their jobs raises your standing with them, through four levels that never drop, even when you die. Each level opens more stock and more jobs and makes their prices a little kinder. Chalky takes special orders at his third level, and Sofie lets you buy on a tab from her second.

The next morning's play-test asked for one thing above all: a box that asks before you spend money. Buying, selling, ordering, handing in jobs, starting a raid and wiping your save all ask first now, with Enter to confirm and Esc to back out. There's a full guide on the [Traders](../../traders/) page.
