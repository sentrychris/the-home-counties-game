## What was there

Each trader had one job at a time: four written ones in order, then odd jobs for something they wanted, all handed in from your stash. Once the written ones were done, every job was much like the last, and a new one only turned up after a raid.

## Now

Jobs come and go on one board shared by all four traders. After every raid, however it went, a new one may come in, and now and then two at once. There are never more than three on offer, and no more than two from any one trader. They don't wait for ever: each is up for a few raids (a rush job for just the one), its card says how many are left, and one that runs out goes, with a note from whoever posted it.

Each job is put together as it comes in: who it's for, what they want, where, how many and what it pays, in that trader's own words. Some still want something brought to them from your stash. The rest have to be done out on the street, in a raid. Those show with your tasks on the gear screen and the pause menu, and pay the moment you've done them, with a JOB COMPLETE box at the top right. There's nothing to accept: anything on the board is live. The traders' own written jobs still turn up among the rest, in order, as your standing allows, and the more they trust you, the better the rest pay.

One promise: never more than one raid running with nothing on offer. If a raid starts with the board empty, a job is waiting the moment you're back.

## Where to find them

Each trader's **Jobs** page lists what they've put up, how many raids each has left and what it pays. The count shows on the page's tab and on the trader's card, and **Jobs on offer** under the traders says how full the board is. Jobs you already had in an old save are moved onto the board.

## What it costs

Nothing while you play. The board is a few dice thrown when a raid ends, and a job done in a raid is one more line in what the game already checks for your tasks.
