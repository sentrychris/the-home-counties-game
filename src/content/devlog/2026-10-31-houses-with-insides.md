## Being built now

This one is still in progress, so there are no pictures of the new insides yet. The picture above is the HQ tent as it was, before it was raised.

The eight houses you can go into on the first map are getting real insides, laid out like a typical British terrace: a front room with a fire surround, a dining room through a pair of open doors, a narrow galley kitchen, a bathroom, and two bedrooms upstairs. The upstairs is open in all of them, so there's a lot more house to search.

Everything is being made at true size: ceilings about two and a half metres up, doors two metres tall, a staircase a metre wide, furniture as big as the real thing. So it doesn't feel like a doll's house with you as a giant inside, the rows these houses stand in have been raised a little to give the rooms their proper height. The rooflines still line up.

There are no working lights, so the houses are lit by whatever comes through the windows.

## Taller tents

The army tents by the Osborne Street checkpoint were too low: you could clip your head walking in. They're being raised so you can walk through them upright.

Pictures when it's done.
