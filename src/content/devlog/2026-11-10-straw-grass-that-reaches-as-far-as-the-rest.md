## Bare ground at the default distance

The game draws grass in two ways. Close up, every tuft is real blades that move in the wind. Further out, each tuft becomes a picture of itself, which costs the graphics card much less. Settings > Graphics sets how far each reaches. The game is designed for 20 m of blades and 60 m of grass, and you can raise that to 40 m and 120 m.

The long green grass has always had its pictures. The burnt straw on the spare ground by the car park, and on the trodden ground in the army compound, didn't. With the grass at 20/60, its blades faded out 12 to 20 metres away and left only the brown earth underneath. From the car park the patch outside the Community House looked bare.

## Now it has pictures of its own

The straw grass now has its own far pictures, taken of the very same straw tufts, so it hands over to them as the long grass does. From the car park the patch looks as it did at 40/120, and it costs next to nothing, as it's only a few hundred square metres of grass.

The pictures are from the car park end of Duncombe Street at 20/60, in the early afternoon sun.
