## The Character tab

The last greyed-out tab on the hideout's top bar is open. **Character** shows you on the left, standing as you do on the end-of-raid screen, in the kit you'd take into a raid. Drag to turn yourself round, and use the mouse wheel or **FACE** to come in close on your face. **SHOW KIT** takes your gear off so you can see just your clothes.

On the right is everything you can change:

- **Ethnicity:** White British, White European, Mixed, South Asian, East Asian, Middle Eastern, Black Caribbean or Black African.
- **Head:** a knitted beanie or not, in one of 12 colours (the scavs' eight, plus cream, bottle green, royal blue and burnt orange), and a beard or clean-shaven.
- **Top:** one of 25. The nine tracksuit tops, with their stripes, then hoodies in grey marl, charcoal, navy, black, olive, maroon, stone, green, mustard, washed red and powder blue, a DPM smock, an MTP top, a desert DPM top, and olive drab and coyote tops.
- **Bottoms:** one of 25. The nine tracksuit bottoms, then dark, blue, stonewash and black jeans, grey, charcoal, black and navy joggers, stone chinos, khaki work trousers, brown cords, DPM, MTP and desert DPM trousers, olive drab trousers and black combats.

Each one is the cloth the scavs wear, drawn on its swatch as it is in the game, with the camouflage patterns and tracksuit stripes. What you've picked is named beside each heading. **RANDOM** picks a whole look for you, and **DEFAULT** goes back to the start: a black hoodie and black joggers, as dark as the near-black tracksuit everyone wore before.

Every pick shows on you straight away and is saved as you make it. Wiping your profile keeps it.

## Where it shows

- **Your own body.** Looking down, you see your own top, trousers and trainers. Your shadow, photo mode and a friend watching you after you've gone down all show the whole of you.
- **Your squad.** Your look goes to the others when you join, and again whenever you change it, so your friends see you as you picked. A friend can now dress like a scav, but their name still shows over their head.
- **The end-of-raid screen.** You stand there in your look and your kit.

The beanie comes off while you've got a helmet on, and goes back on when you take the helmet off. From your own eyes it's only in your shadow, like your head. Your first-person arms keep their black camo sleeves and gloves.

## The scavs

The scavs come from every background now too, in about the mix you'd see in Milton Keynes: most of them White British, then South Asian, White European, Black African, Mixed, and a few East Asian, Middle Eastern and Black Caribbean. In a squad, everyone sees the same scav the same way.

## How it's done

There's still one head shape. Each ethnicity is the character's own skin picture recoloured: every freckle, pore, crease and the redder ears and lips are kept, laid over a new skin tone, so the face reads as the same person in another skin. The hair and beard change colour with it, and for Black Caribbean and Black African the hair is cropped close. The eyes, teeth and mouth stay as they were.
