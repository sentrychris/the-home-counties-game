## The room

The Character tab used to stand you in front of a plain dark backdrop, lit like a studio portrait. Now you stand in a small lock-up that fills the whole page, with the choices panel floating over it:

- **A hanging lamp** in its enamel shade, in view at the top left. It throws a cone of light down through dusty air and casts your long, soft shadow forward across the floor.
- **Wet concrete.** The floor is old grey concrete, damp in patches, with a few puddles of standing water that mirror the lamp and you.
- **Army kit.** Footlockers stacked by your feet with an ammo box on top, a duffel bag, jerry cans, steel shelves of crates, boxes and a field radio, a generator, a reloading bench with a paraffin lantern, a camp bed and a sleeping bag.
- **The building.** A brick back wall, a profiled metal side wall, a steel beam and column, a bulkhead lamp over the shelves, and a roller shutter with grey daylight showing under it.

You're lit by a warm light from the front so your face reads clearly, with a cool edge light from behind. The back of the room is out of focus, like a camera focused on you, so the far lights blur into soft spots.

You stand exactly where you stood before, and everything on the tab works as it did: drag to turn, the mouse wheel or **FACE** to come in close, **SHOW KIT** to see just your clothes. The room is only drawn while the Character tab is open.

## How it's done

The room is built out of the game's own models and pictures, so it adds nothing to the download. The picture of you is now as wide as the screen, with the camera's lens shifted so you stay framed in your column while the room fills in the rest. The dusty air is a small cloud round the lamp's beam, not the whole room: dust everywhere, lit by the lamp, hazed over the floor in front of you and washed out the reflections in the puddles. The room's models start loading in the background as soon as the hideout opens, so the tab doesn't stall the first time you open it.
