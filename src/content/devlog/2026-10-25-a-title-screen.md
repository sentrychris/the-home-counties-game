## The title screen

The game now opens on a stormy street: a wrecked car grown over with ivy, a water tower behind the terraces, and THE HOME COUNTIES in a worn, heavy wordmark. Press any key to start. It's the same backdrop and logo this site uses.

## Wheelie bins

The box bins in the front gardens are now photoreal wheelie bins in Milton Keynes' real colours: black for rubbish, a blue lid for plastic, metal and glass, a red lid for paper and card, and green for food and garden waste.

## No more pop-in

Trees and bushes no longer flicker or pop as you walk. Far-off leaves keep their detail, and each tree's middle-distance copy stands exactly where the tree does.
