## Less weight

The menus were redrawn after a reference picture of what they should feel like: less bulky, less in your face, with lighter letters and more space. The heavy headings are now a lighter condensed face with a little letter spacing, while small print stays bold enough to read. Panels are see-through over a blurred copy of the backdrop, and the Settings, gear and pause screens blur the raid behind them instead of covering it.

## Smaller touches

- Page tabs (each trader's Buy, Sell, Job and Notes, and the Settings pages) are underlined instead of boxed.
- Money has commas everywhere.
- The end-of-raid summary is a list with a picture beside each thing you brought out.
