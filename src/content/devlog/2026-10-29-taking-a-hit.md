## No more red screen

Getting hit used to flash the screen red. Now it feels more like being shot:

- **A flinch.** Each hit jolts your view away from the shooter: hardest to the head, less to the chest, least to the legs, and less when you're aiming.
- **From a side.** The edge of the screen nearest the shooter blurs and dims for a moment, so you can tell where it came from. A grenade does the same from where it went off.
- **Blood loss.** Once you've lost a lot of blood the colour drains away, the edges darken and blur, and it throbs with your heartbeat while you're bleeding. Patch yourself up and it comes back.
- **Going down.** Your sight blurs, greys and fades out and the sound muffles, then the raid summary.

The head-injury blur stays as it was. All of it except the flinch can be switched off with Injury effects in Settings > Picture.
