The weather update brought dips to about 35 frames a second on an RTX 3060 Ti, in some weathers and in firefights. The rule for fixing them: the picture has to stay the same. Nothing was turned down to buy frames. Each fix draws the same picture for less work.

## Drawing

- Rain and overcast skies drew the sun's soft shadows the expensive way. They're now filtered and blurred instead, for 4 to 19 ms less a frame.
- Windows facing away from the sun no longer work out its soft shadows: up to 14 ms less with a low sun.
- Smoke over burnt-out cars and fire drums is lit at the corners of each puff: up to 9 ms less in fog.
- Gunfire smoke, dust and blood puffs skip their see-through pixels before they're lit, which halves their cost with the same look.
- A new **Sun shadows** setting: Auto picks the size of the sun's shadow map for the weather, and Finest keeps the old full size. Under cloud, a smaller map took a fight from 17.4 ms to 12.2 ms on the graphics card.

## Firefights

Big fights also stalled the processor. Sounds are now prepared at start-up, the scavs' route searches run side by side on spare cores, and shots reuse their flashes and tracers. On the test PC, a fight with twelve scavs went from 83 to 165 stutters (frames over 25 ms) down to 18 to 46, and none over 33 ms.

## Your eyes

In rain, overcast and mist your eyes no longer pump darker and lighter as you turn round: the sun's glints off rippled puddles were throwing the exposure. And far windows no longer look black. The glass reflects the sky now, and fades into the mist with the walls.
