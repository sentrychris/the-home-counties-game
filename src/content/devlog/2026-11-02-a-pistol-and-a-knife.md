## The Hi-Power

Press <kbd>3</kbd> to draw the pistol from your holster. It's the old British Army Browning Hi-Power: 9 mm, semi-automatic, one shot each time you pull the trigger, with 13 rounds in the magazine.

A 9 mm round has about a third of an AK round's energy, so each hit does about two thirds of the damage and much less of it gets through armour. There are three kinds, like the AK's: FMJ, armour-piercing and hollow point.

<kbd>R</kbd> drops the magazine and puts in a fresh one. Run it dry and the slide locks back, so the reload ends with your other hand racking it. The grip has been worked over a few times to look right: both hands on it, thumbs forward along the frame, held close and in the middle of your view.

## The Ka-Bar

Press <kbd>4</kbd> for the knife. Left mouse slashes, right mouse stabs, which is slower but hits much harder. It's quiet, it never runs out, and you have to be within arm's length. Hit a wall and it jars off. A plate stops it, and soft armour mostly does too.

## Scavs carry them

About a third of scavs have a pistol, and about a third a knife. A scav that runs dry within 15 metres of you draws its pistol rather than reloading, and if you get right up close and its rifle is no use, it'll go for the knife.

## Where to get them

Carl sells 9 mm and Hi-Power magazines from the start, and usually has a Hi-Power in. Crates turn up 9 mm now and then, and anything a scav was carrying is on its body.

Also in these builds: crouch-walking while looking down no longer pushes your knee through the rifle.
