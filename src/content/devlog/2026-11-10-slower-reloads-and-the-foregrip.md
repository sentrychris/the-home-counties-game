## Reloads at a human pace

The reload animation used to play at one even pace, finished in 3 seconds. It looked hurried, more arcade than real. Now it takes 4.4 seconds with the AK and 4.1 with the M4, and it isn't just slowed down. The old magazine still comes off quickly, but the middle of the reload, putting the empty one away and finding, lining up and seating the full one, takes 1.8 seconds instead of 0.8. The bolt is racked at about its natural speed, and the rifle comes back up quickly at the end. The sounds still land on each step.

Scavs, your squadmates and your own body (what your friends see, and your shadow) reload at the same pace, so a scav changing magazines gives you a moment longer to move. A reload from the backpack still takes 1.5 seconds longer.

## The thumb on the M4

Walking with the M4, your left thumb could sink up to 13 mm into the handguard. The arm animations were made for the AK, whose handguard is narrower and flatter on top. The thumb is now turned out just enough to lie along the M4's handguard, standing, walking and aiming.

## Holding the foregrip

With a vertical foregrip on the M4, your left hand still held the handguard behind it, as if the grip were only there to stop it sliding forward. Now it holds the grip: fingers wrapped round the front, thumb forward along the side, just under the handguard. That goes for first person, looking the rifle over with Alt+I, the end-of-raid screen, your squadmates and the scavs. Through a reload the hand goes from the grip to the magazine and back. Without a foregrip it holds the handguard as before. The foregrip's description in the stash says so too.

The pictures are cloud renders; the thumb and the foregrip close-up have no map around them.
