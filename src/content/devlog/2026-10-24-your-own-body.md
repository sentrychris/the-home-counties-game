## A body

Look down and you see your own chest, legs and feet, and your shadow is a whole person carrying a rifle instead of a floating camera.

## The car park end

The north end of the street by the car park was rebuilt from photos: the Community House with its bay window, a small red-brick cottage with an 1892 plaque, the white-painted house next door, and the spare ground on the corner with fly-tipped boards against the wall and a No Entry sign.

## Junctions

Every junction was checked in play and fixed. Give-way lines run across the side road from kerb to kerb, there's one street name plate on each corner just as you turn in, and lamp posts stand back from the kerb instead of in the road.
