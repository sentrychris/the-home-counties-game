## Real ivy

The ivy on the houses was flat green cards, and some of them hung right across open doorways. It's now real 3D ivy, grown over the walls when the raid loads from scanned ivy leaves, with ragged edges, thick in some places and sparse in others. It keeps off windows, doors and open doorways, so it never blocks a way in.

## Grass

The rebuilt Bagshot Court had left about 320 square metres of flat green ground round it where the old building used to stand. That's all grown over now, with a lawn, long grass along the walls and a worn path from the side door. A bare strip by the gable of the end house on Clifford Avenue got the same.

## A ghost sign

The end house on Clifford Avenue had an odd ochre block stuck to its back. It's now two proper back extensions in the house's own grey render, and its gable carries a faded old painted advert for Sunlight Soap, copied word for word from a real one. The paint is worn away where the render is rough, so it looks like it's been there a hundred years.

## Also

The MK Citizen front page you can find in the street was redone to look like a real copy of the paper.
