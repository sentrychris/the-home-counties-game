What you take in now matters as much as what you bring out.

## Gear slots

You have slots for a helmet, body armour, a tactical rig, a backpack, a knife, a primary and a secondary rifle and a holster, plus four pockets. The rig and the backpack have grids of their own. Gear stays with you between raids, but die and you lose everything you were wearing.

## The hideout

The hideout has two tabs. **Raid** shows the map with its exits and starts you off. **Storage** has your gear on the left and your stash on the right.

## In the raid

Tab opens your gear while the game carries on around you. Esc opens the same screen but pauses, with options to resume, change settings or leave the raid. Reloading takes magazines from your rig first, then your pockets, then your backpack, which is slower. Drop something and it lands as itself, and a backpack keeps what's in it.

## Scavs wear it too

Scavs now go out in rigs, helmets, body armour and backpacks, and when you search one you see their slots, the same as yours.
