## Worn menus

The menus now look like they belong in the game's world: dark, scuffed panels, chipped headings and handwritten notes. The Raid tab shows the street as a map print taped to the wall, with notes scrawled on it, green running men at the exits, a north arrow and a scale. Your kit is summed up on the right: every slot, what you're carrying, what's at risk and how much it weighs. Character, Traders, Map and Tasks sit on the bar as greyed-out placeholders for now.

This site borrows the same look.

## Graphics settings

- **Anti-aliasing:** FXAA, SMAA, MSAA from 2x to 8x, TAA, combinations of them, or FSR 2 at full size. SMAA, TAA and FSR 2 also smooth the edges of leaves, which MSAA can't.
- **Ambient occlusion** from Medium to Ultra.
- **Texture sharpness** up to 16x, now the default.

## Sun glare and head injuries

Looking towards the sun now gives a soft glare that only shows where the sun actually peeks through. A head injury gets to your eyes: below 60% head health your vision blurs in waves, and below about 30% you see double. A round stopped by your helmet stuns you for a few seconds, with muffled hearing and ringing ears. Both effects can be switched off under Settings > Picture.
