## Your rifle

Each shot of your AK is now a dry, close shot with one of four 7.62 reflections layered over it, the sound bouncing back off the houses. Indoors it gets a room's tail instead.

## Everyone else's

Other people's rifles sound different close up, at middle distance and far away, and different again from inside a building. Sound also travels at its real speed: a shot or a grenade 300 metres away reaches you nearly a second after it happened, so you can judge distance by ear.

## Rounds going past

A round that passes close to you cracks, louder the closer it came, in four bands out to ten metres, and one within three metres flies by with a whizz. Rounds hitting things sound like what they hit: brick, concrete, metal, wood, glass, earth or flesh. A round that glances off at a shallow angle can ricochet away with a whine. The frag grenade's bang comes from the same pack, close and medium recordings blended by distance.
