## You can see what you're doing

Healing used to happen off screen while a bar filled. Now the item is in your hands for as long as it takes, and you see it used:

- **First aid kit** (3 seconds): the pouch comes up in your left hand, you unzip it, take out a gauze pad and press it on.
- **Bandage** (3 seconds): tear the packet, take out the roll, drop the packet and wind the dressing round the back of your hand.
- **Painkillers** (2 seconds): pop a capsule out of the blister and take it, head tipping back.
- **Surgical kit** (10 seconds): the open trauma case on your palm, the needle holder out of the lid, three stitches, then a dressing.

The rifle goes down while you work and comes back up when you're done. A few rounds of play-testing put the tools properly in the fingers, firmed up the wrists, and stopped the blister pack going through a thumb.

## Alt+I

**Alt+I** in a raid lifts your rifle and turns it to show you its right side, then its left, as in Tarkov and Arena Breakout. Handy for checking what you've fitted.
