## Last orders

The plain brick box at the end of the Station footpath is now The Park, a traditional pub built from a piece of concept art: cream render, a slate roof and chimneys, green and gold fascias, a hanging sign on the corner and a beer garden behind a wall of brick piers and railings. It stands on the corner of Duncombe Street.

It hasn't been shut long, but it's been looted. You can walk in through the bar, the back rooms and the yard behind. A small car park round the back stops at the boundary wall, so the pub itself sits inside the map, and a little side road with a pavement now runs between the pub and the houses next door.
