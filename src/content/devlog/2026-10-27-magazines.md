## Mags as items

Until now the AK simply had thirty rounds in it. Magazines are now items of their own, as in Tarkov and Arena Breakout. Each one holds thirty rounds of one kind, takes two squares, and shows its calibre, capacity and what's loaded in it.

- **Loading:** drag a pile of rounds onto a magazine, or right-click it > **Load with...**, which lists every kind of ammo you have with how many in brackets.
- **Fitting:** drag a magazine onto a rifle, or right-click the rifle > **Load magazine...** to pick one. A rifle with no magazine in it shows without one in its picture.
- **In a raid:** R swaps to your fullest magazine of the same ammo, from your rig and pockets first (a backpack takes longer). T swaps to the next kind of ammo. The old magazine goes where the new one came from.

Spare magazines are too long for the 1x1 pockets, so a rig matters more now. A new character starts with a loaded AK and two full magazines in the rig, and older saves are handed three empty magazines. Scavs carry one to three spares, and Carl sells them.

The old worn AK model was retired at the same time, so every rifle in the game is now the detailed one.
