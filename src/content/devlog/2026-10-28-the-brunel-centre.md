## A shopping centre

The plain block next to the old supermarket is now the Brunel Centre, built from a piece of concept art: a curtain wall of black glass with a gold name and a grid of shop signs behind it. The shops are made-up names, sun-faded and peeling. Some panes are cracked, some are missing, and you can see into the empty units behind the broken ones.

## The yard and the shutters

Between the centre and the supermarket runs a service yard behind black palisade fencing: big bins, pallets, abandoned trolleys, weeds in the cracks, a lamp post and an alley. The supermarket's roller shutters were redone at the same time as real slats of metal that catch the light, with graffiti sprayed across them.

A build later the wall was moved to line up properly, the verge in front was let grow over, and a stray kerb that ran across the car park was taken away. The whole thing costs about 0.4 ms on the test PC.
