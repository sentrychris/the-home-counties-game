## Swapping and opening

Drop an item onto a slot or square that's already taken and the two swap: a scav's AK onto your Primary gives him yours, a bandage onto a pocket with a watch in it puts the watch where the bandage was. It works in raid, in the hideout and looting with your squad.

Double-click a rig or backpack, or right-click it and pick **Open**, and it opens in a window of its own. Put a rig in a small pack and the pack in a rucksack and each one opens inside the last. A full rig put in a backpack keeps everything in it.

## Little things

- **X** drops whatever's under the mouse in the raid gear screens, and **C** toggles crouch.
- Right-click menus open beside the mouse.
- The stash has an **AUTO-ORGANISE** button that packs everything tight in a sensible order. Otherwise it remembers where you put things.

## Walking through the dead

Dead scavs and squadmates no longer block you. You walk straight through a body, and it can still be searched with F where it fell. Their bodies now lie along the ground the way they fell, so rounds and eyes still meet them.
