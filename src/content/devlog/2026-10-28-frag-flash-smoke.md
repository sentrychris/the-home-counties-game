## Three grenades

- **M67 frag.** A 4.2 second fuse, a blast within 4 metres, and 900 fragments that strike whatever they can see. Walls, cars and corners save you; helmets and plates stop most of it.
- **M84 flashbang.** Whites you out for up to five seconds if you're close and looking at it, with your ears ringing for longer. Scavs are blinded the same way and drop whatever they were about to throw.
- **M18 smoke (grey).** Pours smoke for 55 seconds into a soft wall about nine metres across that scavs can't see through, then hangs a while longer.

## Carrying and throwing

Grenades live in your rig or pockets, one square each, not your backpack. In a raid, **G** brings one up (G again for the next kind). Hold the left mouse button to pull the pin and wind up, and let go to throw. The right mouse button lobs it underhand instead. 1 or 2 puts it away with the pin still in.

## The scavs

Scavs carry them too. They run for cover from a frag or flash they see land, lob one now and then at where they last saw you, and often pop smoke before they fall back. In a squad, everyone sees the same grenade.

## Smoke like it should be

The first smoke was a bit thin, so it was redone: it billows out of the can into a pale grey wall head-high within a few seconds, solid down to the ground, its top fraying into haze. The sun lights its near side and leaves the far side darker. At its thickest it costs about 3 ms a frame. See the [Grenades](../../guides/grenades/) guide for the details.
