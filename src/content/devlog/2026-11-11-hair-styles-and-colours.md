## Hair

You can pick your hair now. The Character tab has a new **Hair** row under Ethnicity, with its style and colour named beside it ("Ginger afro"):

- **Five styles.** **SHORT** is the short hair you had before. **BUZZ CUT** is cropped close to the scalp, **SHAVED** a bare head, **CURLS** a short crop of tight curls, and **AFRO** a big round afro.
- **Ten colours.** Black, dark brown, brown, light brown, dark blond, blond, strawberry blond, ginger, auburn and grey.
- **All of it matches.** The colour goes on your beard, eyebrows, eyelashes and stubble as well as your hair. A shaved head keeps only a faint shadow of it.
- **Hats and helmets.** Curls and an afro go flat under a beanie or a helmet: you wear a buzz cut in the same colour while it's on, and your hair is back as soon as it comes off.

The look you had before keeps the hair it had. You're seen with your new hair everywhere you were before: your own shadow and photo mode, your squadmates' screens and the end-of-raid screen.

## The scavs

Scavs have hair to match. Most have short hair or a buzz cut and some shave their heads. Dark brown, black and brown are the commonest colours, with some light brown, blond, ginger, auburn and grey. Curls and the odd afro turn up mostly on Black and some mixed-race scavs. Nobody wears big hair under a hat or a helmet.

## How it's done

There were no free hair models that fit the game's character (nearly all are cartoon or long styles), so the hair is made in the game. The afro and the curls are drawn as layers grown out from the scalp: forty for the afro, fourteen for the curls. Each layer shows only where the coils are still thick enough that far out, so the hair is dense next to the head and fuzzy at the edge, and from further off the coils blend into a soft mass. The short hair and the beard are the character's own hair picture in the new colour. The scalp, eyebrows and stubble painted on the skin are recoloured by the skin itself, keeping their shading.
