## A voice

Your character used to be silent. Now there's a voice, recorded for the game rather than generated: a grunt when a round hits you (not every time), groans and coughs every so often while you're hurt and haven't healed, and a gasp when you run out of stamina. They're used sparingly, so they mean something when you hear them. Painkillers quieten the groans.

## Blood

It wasn't always obvious when you'd started bleeding. Now blood spatters across your view from the side you were hit, one patch for each wound that's bleeding, soft and out of focus as if it were on your face rather than on the screen. The middle of the view stays clear so you can still fight. It fades a few seconds after the bleeding stops.

The spatter is made from photos of real blood, and it's drawn in the same pass as the other hit effects, so it costs next to nothing.
