## A closed gate

The east end of Osborne Street, by Bagshot Court, was a bare stretch of road ending at the map's edge. It's now a military vehicle checkpoint, built from a concept picture: a heavy gate across the road, shut, with a watchtower either side under camouflage netting, HESCO walls running off to the gardens and down the side street, sandbags, a guard hut, cones and signs. The gate stays shut because there's nothing behind it but the edge of the map, and that's exactly how it should feel: the way out of the Zone is closed.

The boundary was moved back at the same time, so the last three cottages' front gardens are open to walk through.

It came out of a single afternoon's work and cost under a millisecond on the test PC. It was rebuilt a good deal more seriously later on: see [A proper checkpoint](../2026-10-30-a-proper-checkpoint/).
