## Windows and doors

Until now the houses had their windows painted on. This build cuts real holes in the walls and fits proper joinery into them: uPVC casements, timber sashes, front doors with glass and letterboxes, all with depth and frames that catch the light. Bay windows were rebuilt, porches were rebuilt, and the backs of the terraces got fifty little extensions with lean-to roofs, back doors and soil pipes. A boarded-up window no longer has an open sash floating in front of it.

## The building site

The north edge of the map used to be a plain hoarding with the Station footpath extract at one end. It now looks like somewhere work stopped in a hurry: a fenced compound in the car park corner with stacked site cabins, a container, portable toilets and a lighting tower, scaffolding against the hoarding, a skip and a mixer, the road closed off with barriers, and two army tents on the supermarket car park. Over the hoarding stands a half-built concrete frame in green sheeting with a tower crane swinging its jib out over the street.

All of this costs well under a millisecond on the test PC.
