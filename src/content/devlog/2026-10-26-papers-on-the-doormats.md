Nobody in The Home Counties tells you what happened here. The street does, if you look.

## Things to read

- **Leaflets** lie on the doormats of the open houses.
- **Cards** are still up in some front windows.
- **Notices** are pasted on doors: weathered sheets saying the property was checked, and how many people were still inside.
- **Logs** on clipboards at both army checkpoints record what the soldiers saw there.

Walk up to one and press F to read it.

## Scav marks

Scavs mark the houses they've been through with spray paint: red and black crosses, EMPTY and NOTHING LEFT, with drips and overspray.

## Also

Sixteen pieces of gear now have a line about them when you point at them, and a tall mast to the north-west, with a blinking red light, shows over the rooftops.
