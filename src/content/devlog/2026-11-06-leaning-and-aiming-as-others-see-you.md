## Where you're really looking

Leaning round a car in photo mode, your character looked as if it was aiming into the car, with the rifle tipped up on a diagonal, while your own view looked straight past it. Your body took its hold from the animation clips, which carry the rifle about 20 degrees off to one side, and leaning only bent it at the waist, which tipped the rifle over far more than your view tips. Your squadmates had the same lean.

Now your body and your squadmates are posed from what each of you actually sees. The rifle points exactly where you look and leans over only as far as your view does. At the ready it's held level in the shoulder, under your chin. When you aim it comes up to your cheek: your head bows onto the stock, your eye sits just behind the sights, and the stock stays in your shoulder.

## Leaning

When you lean, you bend at the waist and your hips shift a little the same way, until your head is out where your eyes are. So the head a scav or a friend sees round a corner is the one that can be hit, and from in front you look like someone peeking, not someone falling over. The pistol and knife lean the same way.

## In co-op

Your squadmates are told when you aim, so they see you bring the rifle up to your cheek too.
