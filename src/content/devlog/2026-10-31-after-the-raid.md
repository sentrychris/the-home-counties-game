## How did it go?

When a raid ends you now see yourself on the left, standing in the kit you wore, and a quiet panel on the right: **RAID COMPLETE** or **RAID FAILED**, then extracted, killed in action or missing in action.

Underneath are five numbers: how long you were in, how many scavs you killed, what you looted, how much damage you took and how many times you healed. If you didn't make it out, "value looted" becomes "value lost": what the kit you took in was worth.

The raid stops behind the screen, so nobody keeps shooting at you while you read it, and it sits over a blurred copy of the title screen's street.

## Longer raids

Raids now last 18 minutes, up from 12. It gives you time to search a house properly, or to wait a scav out.
