## Bagshot Court

The retirement flats on the corner of Clifford Avenue and Osborne Street were a plain block. They're now rebuilt from four photos of the real building: red brick with buff bands at sill and floor level, grey hipped roofs, white fascias, black gutters, a low front wall with railings, a gabled back wing and a timber porch. Walk in by the back porch and there's a hallway, a lounge and a staircase up to two more flats, with a crate waiting upstairs. It's the first building in the game with an upstairs. The car park round the side has six cars in it.

## The back gardens

A few buildings that stuck out of the back gardens behind Duncombe Street and Water Eaton Road were taken away and their plots grown over, and one more garden house was added where it belonged. Open front doors now swing far enough that the door doesn't sink into the wall, and narrow houses keep their front window clear of the door. Eight houses can be walked into, and there are now 20 crates across the map.
