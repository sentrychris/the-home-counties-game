## A slimmer handguard

The M4's handguard, with its rail covers, was 83 mm across: half as wide again as the AK's (54 mm). Your left hand holds both rifles the same way, so on the M4 its fingers sank into the sides. The handguard is now 60 mm across, a little wider than the AK's, and your hand sits a little higher up under it. The light on the left rail and the foregrip underneath moved in with it.

## Red dots over the front sight

Both red dots stood with the dot at the height of the iron sights, so when you aimed, the M4's front sight stood in the middle of the window. They now sit higher: the front sight's ears come only about a third of the way up the window (a "lower third" mount), and the dot sits above them, with a clear view. The tube red dot's own mount is taller, and the AK's open red dot, which went straight onto the M4's top rail, now stands on a riser there. On the AK it's as it was, and the 4x scope didn't need it.

## Reloading

The arm animations were made for the AK, whose magazine rocks out of the rifle about its front. The M4's magazine was rocking out the same way, so for a moment it swung through the sides of the well. The last post said it came straight down; it didn't. Now it does: it slides straight down the well's full depth and only turns away with your hand once it's clear, and the new one comes back the same way, lined up under the well and pushed straight up into it.

The stash pictures of the M4 and the tube red dot are redrawn to match. Nothing else has changed. The pictures are cloud renders; the reload is seen from beside the rifle, with no map around it.
